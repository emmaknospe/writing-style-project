## WHAT HAPPENED FEDERALLY

**H.R. 1** — "the so-called One Big Beautiful Bill." Signed federally; the largest
cuts to Medicaid in the program's history.

- Cuts Medicaid by **$1 trillion over 10 years** nationwide
- Virginia's share of the loss: **$1.8–2.6 billion per year at minimum, beginning
  2028**
- Cuts **$187 billion** from federal **SNAP** over the next decade
- Weakens **Medicare's authority to negotiate prescription drug prices** — which she
  voted to establish while serving in Congress
- Separately, the Trump Administration announced (week of 7/27) it will **end a
  Medicare Part D subsidy program** used by **nearly 690,000 Virginia seniors** to
  keep prescription drug premiums low

## WHAT VIRGINIA IS DOING — THE BUDGET

**Nearly $1 billion** in the 2026–2028 biennial budget to contend with the worst of
the impacts:

- **$350 million** — new **Medicaid Reserve Fund**, to prepare for potential federal
  Medicaid cuts and protect access for the hundreds of thousands who rely on the
  program
- **$150 million** — new **premium assistance program**, targeted, for Virginians
  losing access to affordable coverage through the **ACA Marketplace**

Her line: "Our new state budget includes $1 billion to help shield kids, families, and
our most vulnerable neighbors from the worst of these cuts."

## RURAL HOSPITALS

- The **Joint Commission on Healthcare** issued a report identifying **hospitals most
  at risk of closure** because of the federal healthcare cuts.
- **VCU Health Tappahannock Hospital** was named in that report. She visited it
  **8/14/26**.
- Her framing: "Our rural hospitals are the backbone of healthcare across so much of
  Virginia."
- Pressures named by the administration, consistently: **provider shortages, workforce
  challenges, long travel times for patients, growing financial pressures.**

**Rural Health Transformation initiative**
- **$189.5 million in year-one funding**, multi-year effort to strengthen and
  modernize rural healthcare systems
- **First grant: $127,500** to **Virginia Highlands Community College**, Abingdon
  (announced 5/21/26) — expands the **LPN program**: more course offerings, more
  student capacity, instructional support
- Sits inside the **Homegrown Health Heroes** initiative — rural healthcare workforce
  pipelines built through public education, community colleges, and local providers
- **Heidi Hertz**, Director of Rural Health Transformation: success looks like
  "lasting partnerships, rural Virginians can access quality care closer to home, and
  rural healthcare systems are stronger and more resilient for generations to come"
