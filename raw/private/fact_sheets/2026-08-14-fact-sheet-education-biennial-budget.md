# EDUCATION INVESTMENTS — 2026–2028 BIENNIAL BUDGET

One-pager · 8/14/26 · for school-visit and back-to-school use

---

## TOP LINE

**$2 billion in new education funding** in the Commonwealth's biennial bipartisan
budget — **the largest investment in public education in Virginia history.**

Budget finalized **June 29, 2026**, when the General Assembly accepted all fourteen of
the Governor's amendments.

## THE NUMBERS

**K-12**
- $2B new education funding (top line)
- **4 percent raises** for teachers **and** school support staff — *each year* of the
  biennium. Support staff are in the number; say so.
- **$519 million** in grants to fund **school construction** — modernized, safe
  buildings

**Higher education — $349.7 million total**
- **$82.5M** to bring down tuition costs
- **$65M** in new financial aid
- **$50M** to support **HBCUs**
