# TAPPAHANNOCK — EDUCATION + RURAL HEALTHCARE

## PURPOSE

One day, two anchors: the **record education investment** during the first week of
school, and a **rural hospital named on the at-risk list**. The pairing is the
argument. Historic state investment on one side of town, federal cuts threatening the
hospital on the other. Don't let the day get read as two separate visits.

## SEQUENCE

| | |
| --- | --- |
| AM | **James H. Cary Intermediate School** — students, teachers, staff; educator roundtable on the record $2B |
| Midday | Local stops — Java Jacks, Tappahannock Art Gallery, DAW Theater, 1710 Tavern |
| PM | **VCU Health Tappahannock Hospital** |
| PM | **WRAR-FM** — over-the-air to the region |
| Late | **Tappahannock-Essex Volunteer Fire Department** — sit-down with firefighters |

Sec. Education **Dr. Jeffery Smith** travels for the school portion.

## BLOCK 1 — SCHOOL

Roundtable is with educators, on the biennial budget. Numbers she'll want at hand:

- **$2B** new education funding — largest investment in public education in Virginia
  history
- **4% raises** for teachers *and support staff*, each year
- **$519M** school construction grants
- Higher ed **$349.7M** — $82.5M tuition, $65M financial aid, $50M HBCUs

Personal frame, first week of school: mom of three daughters in Virginia public
schools. It has worked every time; use it.

## BLOCK 2 — HOSPITAL

**VCU Health Tappahannock Hospital was included in the Joint Commission on
Healthcare's report of hospitals most at risk of closure because of the federal
healthcare cuts.** This is the reason we are here and it should be said plainly.

What we bring:
- **Nearly $1B** in the biennial budget to contend with the worst of the H.R. 1
  impacts
- **$350M** Medicaid Reserve Fund
- **$150M** Marketplace premium assistance
- Rural Health Transformation — **$189.5M** year one

**President Josh Hammond** hosts. His framing in advance: the hospital's success rests
on its people, VCU Health System investment, and regional partners — "our headwinds are
strong, but our team's dedication and our health system's commitment to this community
is stronger." He is not asking us to fix it; he is asking to be seen. Match that.

Sec. HHR **Marvin B. Figueroa** on the record for this trip: federal changes are
creating uncertainty for rural providers, and the budget money exists precisely to
mitigate.

**Tone caution.** "At risk of closure" is a real phrase about a real hospital in a town
of people who use it. Say what is threatening it and what the Commonwealth is doing.
Do not say or imply the hospital is closing.

## BLOCK 3 — FIRE DEPARTMENT

Listening — challenges they face, how the Commonwealth can support them. She has a
concrete deliverable here: the budget amendment she requested and the General Assembly
accepted providing **lifesaving cancer screenings for Virginia firefighters**. Not
widely known in the volunteer companies. Worth surfacing.

## LIKELY LOCAL PRESS

- *Is Tappahannock's hospital going to close?* — Not something we predict. It was
  named at risk because of federal cuts; here is what the state is putting behind it.
- *Does the state money replace what Washington cut?* — No, and we shouldn't pretend
  otherwise. Standing language: we cannot undo all the harm, we will use every tool.
- *Teacher pay — is 4% enough given costs?* — It's each year, it includes support
  staff, and it sits alongside the largest education investment in state history.
- *Energy bills* — likely, and fine. Consumption tax, transmission order, RGGI credit.

## STANDING NOTE

This is the template we want to repeat in other regions: school in the morning, at-risk
provider in the afternoon, local businesses in between, regional radio, first
responders to close. Advance should build the next two the same way.
