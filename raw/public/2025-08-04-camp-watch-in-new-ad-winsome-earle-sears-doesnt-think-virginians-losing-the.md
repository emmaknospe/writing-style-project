On Friday, Sears Refused to “Participate” in Discussion on Trump’s Firing of Virginia Workers & Assault on Virginia’s Economy During a CNN Interview

Starting Today, This New Ad will Target Households Across Northern Virginia

RICHMOND, Va. — The Spanberger for Governor campaign today released a new digital ad, criticizing Winsome Earle-Sears for putting loyalty to Donald Trump first, ahead of what’s best for Virginia’s workforce and economy as Virginians continue to suffer from Trump’s firing of thousands of Virginians.

The ad, titled “Real Issues,” highlights a Friday CNN interview, during which Sears refused to answer questions about her support for the Trump Administration’s firing of Virginia workers. Sears said she was “not going to participate” in discussions about Virginians losing their livelihoods and wanted to talk about “real issues.”

Starting today, the ad will be targeted to thousands of households across Northern Virginia.

In contrast, Abigail Spanberger has consistently warned that the Trump Administration’s reckless actions are hurting Virginia families and businesses — including in a Fox News op-ed last month. Spanberger also recently announced her “Growing Virginia Plan,” which is focused on boosting Virginia’s economy, protecting Virginia jobs, and creating opportunities for the next generation of Virginians.

Click here to watch Spanberger’s new ad, and read the transcript of “Real Issues” below:

—

V/O: Trump job cuts are hitting Virginia really hard, but Winsome Earle-Sears doesn’t think it’s a real problem.

Manu Raju: Do you support the President’s purge of the federal workforce, yes or no?

Winsome Earle-Sears: Oh, you know — okay. If this is the way you want to go, then go ahead, but I’m just not going to participate, because I want to talk about real issues.

V/O: Really? Not a real issue that thousands of Virginians are losing their jobs? Not a real issue that communities are suffering?

Sears: I want to talk about real issues.

V/O: Sears supports Trump, not Virginians. And that’s a real issue.

V/O: Winsome Earle-Sears: so far right, she’s wrong for Virginia.

BACKGROUND

Winsome Earle-Sears has repeatedly supported the Trump Administration’s attacks on Virginia jobs and dismissed the concerns of Virginians worried about their livelihoods.
