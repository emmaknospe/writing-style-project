Last Week, Northam, Wexton, Connolly, Beyer, Luria, & Many Virginia State and Local Officials Announced Their Endorsements

HENRICO, Va. — More federal, state, and local officials from across the Commonwealth today endorsed U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia.

Today’s endorsements underscore support for Spanberger at all levels of Virginia government and demonstrate the early momentum behind her campaign.

The following Virginia elected officials today announced their support for Spanberger to serve as Virginia’s next Governor.

Spanberger thanked these elected officials for their support in the early days of her 2025 campaign.

“Since launching our campaign last week, I’ve been humbled by the tremendous amount of support we’ve received from all corners of our Commonwealth — at all levels,” said Spanberger. “As Governor, I look forward to working with these public servants to support our schools, create family-sustaining jobs, and keep our communities safe.”

BACKGROUND

U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger (VA-07) is serving her third term in the U.S. House of Representatives. In Congress, she serves on the U.S. House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence and the U.S. House Agriculture Committee.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — tracking narcotics traffickers and working money laundering cases — and as a CIA case officer — working undercover on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are raising three school-aged daughters in Virginia.
