By Staff

Save the Children Action Network last week endorsed former 7th District Rep. Abigail Spanberger for governor of Virginia. The group cited her commitment to early childhood education, child nutrition and bipartisan problem-solving, according to a campaign release.

“Save the Children Action Network proudly endorses Abigail Spanberger for governor of Virginia,” said Randy Moore, SCAN’s Virginia state manager. “With her proven track record of bipartisan leadership and deep commitment to early learning and child nutrition, she is the champion our children need. Spanberger understands that quality early learning is the foundation for success, and that no child can learn on an empty stomach. She is exactly the leader Virginia families deserve at this critical moment.”

Children are facing the consequences of program cuts in Washington, D.C., said the Democratic nominee for governor.

“As the next Governor of Virginia, I will make sure our children have every opportunity to succeed in school – starting with high-quality, affordable early childhood education,” Spanberger said. “I’m grateful for Save the Children Action Network’s support, and I look forward to building a better future for all of Virginia’s parents and children as the Commonwealth’s next Governor.”

Save the Children Action Network announced it is preparing “a robust electoral campaign” in support of Spanberger for Governor and said it plans to announce a bipartisan slate of House of Delegates endorsements later this year.

The D.C.-based lobbyist group advocates for expanding early childhood education in the U.S., preventing child hunger in the U.S. and globally, and protecting children around the world from harm in times of crisis.
