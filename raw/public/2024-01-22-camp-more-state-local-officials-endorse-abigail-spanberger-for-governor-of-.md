HENRICO, Va. — Today, more Virginia state and local leaders endorsed U.S. Representative Abigail Spanberger to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia.

These new endorsements reflect Spanberger’s appeal across the Commonwealth of Virginia, as well as her demonstrated commitment to addressing the key issues facing Virginia’s communities.

The following Virginia elected officials today endorsed Abigail Spanberger to serve as Virginia’s next Governor:

Mary Sue Terry, Former Attorney General of Virginia

Anthony F. “Tony” Troy, Former Attorney General of Virginia

Former Sen. George L. Barker, Virginia Senate, District 39

Del. Laura Jane Cohen, Virginia House of Delegates, District 15

Colette Wallace McEachin, Commonwealth’s Attorney, City of Richmond

John David Smith, Jr., Mayor of Winchester

Frankie Edmondson, MCR, Commissioner of the Revenue, Portsmouth

Supervisor Andres Jimenez, Fairfax County Board of Supervisors

Supervisor Dalia Palchik, Fairfax County Board of Supervisors

Sonia Vasquez Luna, Councilwoman, City of Manassas

Shaun Peet, Councilman, Town of Dumfries

Sammoto Dabney, Councilman, Town of Quantico

Mateo Dunne, Fairfax County School Board

Robyn Lady, Fairfax County School Board

Melanie Meren, Fairfax County School Board

Rachna Sizemore Heizer, Fairfax County School Board

Justin Wilk, Prince William County School Board

Nicole T. Cole, Spotsylvania County School Board
