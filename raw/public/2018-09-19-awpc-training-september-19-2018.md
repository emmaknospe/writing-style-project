SPANBERGER:
I'm Abigail Spanberger. Virginia's economy is strong and we need to keep it growing by creating more vocational training and apprenticeships so you can get a good job and earn a good living whether you have a four-year college degree or not. And better job training means more skilled workers. That helps businesses keep growing creating more opportunities. I approve this message because more training means better paying jobs that's the key to our future.
