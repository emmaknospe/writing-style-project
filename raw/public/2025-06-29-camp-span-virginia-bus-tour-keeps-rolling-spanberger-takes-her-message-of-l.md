McLean, Va. — On Friday, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger’s “Span Virginia Bus Tour” continued with stops including Warren County, Leesburg, Falls Church, Arlington, and McLean.

Spanberger met with local elected leaders, visited local businesses, and listened to Virginians discuss the issues that matter most to them throughout the stops. Spanberger emphasized her commitment to always putting Virginia first — including through her “Affordable Virginia Plan” to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs.

WARREN COUNTY

To kick off the seventh day of the “Span Virginia Bus Tour,” Spanberger stopped by Apple House in Linden to listen to Virginians about the issues that matter to them most and outline her vision for Virginia’s future — including working to make Virginia’s public schools the best in America and lowering Virginians’ costs at the pharmacy counter, in the housing market, and on their electricity bills.

LEESBURG

Spanberger, along with state Senator Ghazala Hashmi and former Delegate Jay Jones, the Democratic nominees for Lieutenant Governor and Attorney General, met with former Rep. Jennifer Wexton, Rep. Suhas Subramanyam, and local elected leaders before visiting several local businesses. Spanberger spoke with small business owners, listening to the challenges they face and how the chaos coming out of Washington is impacting Main Street Virginia.

FALLS CHURCH

Spanberger, joined by Hashmi and Jones, then headed to Falls Church and visited several Vietnamese-American small businesses at the Eden Center. Spanberger recognized the important contributions of Virginia’s vibrant Vietnamese-American community and listened to Virginians discuss economic development, entrepreneurship, and public safety priorities.

ARLINGTON

Spanberger, Hashmi, and Jones then headed to Freddie’s, a historic space for the LGBTQ+ community in Arlington, to rally with Virginians and celebrate Pride alongside Rep. Don Beyer and other local leaders.  As Governor, Spanberger will always stand up for Virginians’ fundamental freedoms and make sure the Commonwealth is inclusive and welcoming for all.

MCLEAN

Spanberger ended the day by addressing Network NOVA’s Women’s Summit and was joined by Senator Tim Kaine, Hashmi, Jones, and elected leaders from across the Commonwealth. During her remarks, Spanberger discussed the stakes of this year’s election and the need to elect leaders that reflect Virginia values.

BACKGROUND

Spanberger announced the launch of her “Span Virginia Bus Tour” last week at a rally at her high school alma mater, J.R. Tucker High School.

Along the “Span Virginia Bus Tour,” Spanberger is touting her plan to lower costs for Virginians — the “Affordable Virginia Plan.” Over the last month, she has held events across Virginia outlining this vision to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

Spanberger served three terms in the U.S. House of Representatives from January 2019 to January 2025. During her three terms in Congress, Spanberger was consistently ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — working narcotics and money laundering cases. She then served as a CIA case officer — working on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters in Virginia public schools.
