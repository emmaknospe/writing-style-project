“Tonight’s Decisive Victory Is a Preview of What’s to Come on November 4”

NORFOLK, Va. — After Abigail Spanberger’s decisive win at this evening’s statewide gubernatorial debate at Norfolk State University — where she laid out her clear vision to put Virginia first, Spanberger for Governor Campaign Manager Samson Signori released the following statement.

“The contrast on the debate stage could not have been more clear. Virginians saw their next Governor in Abigail Spanberger — a steady leader who will listen, is ready to work for them, and will always stand up for the Commonwealth. Winsome Earle-Sears showed that she won’t listen to Virginians and will always put President Trump, not Virginians, first.

“Tonight, Virginians saw Abigail Spanberger’s clear focus on lowering their healthcare, housing, and energy costs. Virginians saw that Abigail is a leader who will protect their jobs, stand up for Virginia’s economy, and strengthen Virginia’s schools. And Abigail made clear that she will keep Virginians safe. As a former federal law enforcement officer, her dedication to protecting our communities guides her leadership — and earned her the endorsement of the Virginia Police Benevolent Association.

“What Virginians also saw tonight is that Winsome Earle-Sears cannot defend her extreme record without coming unglued — and she will not separate herself from President Trump and his chaos. No matter the cost for Virginia, Sears will fall in line behind the Trump Administration and politicians in Washington who are firing Virginians and raising their healthcare, housing, and energy costs. Abigail is the only candidate in this race who will always put Virginia first — and Virginians saw proof of that tonight.

“The momentum has been on Abigail’s side throughout this race — and tonight’s decisive victory is a preview of what’s to come on November 4.”
