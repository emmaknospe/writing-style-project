Virginia’s Voter Registration Deadline is October 24

Spanberger: “I Encourage All Eligible Virginians to Get Registered, Double Check Their Registration Status, and Make a Plan to Vote Early or on Election Day”

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today — on National Voter Registration Day — encouraged all eligible Virginians to check their registration status or get registered to vote.

“With early voting in Virginia’s general elections beginning on Friday, I encourage all eligible Virginians to get registered, double check their registration status, and make a plan to vote early or on Election Day. This week, Virginians across the Commonwealth will start heading to the polls with an opportunity to choose leaders who will put them first, get to work to bring down costs for Virginia families, and build a stronger future for all Virginians. I hope Virginians will join me in heeding the reminder that is National Voter Registration Day and take a moment to make sure they are prepared to exercise their right to vote this election season.”
