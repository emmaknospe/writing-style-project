RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today released the following statement on Independence Day 2025.

“Every year on Independence Day, Virginians proudly honor the principles of liberty and democracy that inspired our nation. Nearly a quarter millennia ago, our Founding Fathers declared independence from a monarch and sought to build a lasting republic guided by the consent of the people. The responsibility to safeguard the American values enshrined at our nation’s founding now rests on our shoulders.

“Today, Virginians recognize the generations of patriots who toiled and sacrificed to deliver on this sacred obligation and build a more perfect union. We also honor the selfless service of Virginia Veterans who defended liberty at home and abroad — both those who returned home and those buried under white crosses on distant shores. And we express our gratitude to the servicemembers and first responders who continue to keep us safe during this holiday.

“Next year, Virginia will join our fellow Americans in marking 250 years of independence. As the next Governor of Virginia, I am committed to ensuring we celebrate the occasion with the pride and patriotism our Commonwealth has earned for its unique place in American history. And this year, I hope all Virginians enjoy a restful and joyous holiday with friends and loved ones.”
