Spanberger: “The Trump Administration’s Sledgehammer Approach to Governing Is Hurting Virginia”

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger continues to focus on protecting Virginians’ jobs and standing up to chaos in Washington that is making life more expensive for Virginia families.

Following the U.S. Department of Transportation’s reckless decision to withdraw nearly $40 million in funding for the City of Norfolk to support Virginia’s nation-leading offshore wind industry, Spanberger slammed the Trump Administration for jeopardizing hundreds of new jobs in the region and undermining new energy development — all while Virginia families’ energy bills are rising.

Read more below about what Virginians in Hampton Roads are seeing about Spanberger’s focus on standing up for Virginia.

—

13News Now [Norfolk]: Trump admin cuts $40M Norfolk offshore wind project; Spanberger calls move a blow to Virginia

Democratic candidate for governor Abigail Spanberger (D-Va.) condemned the move, calling it a “sledgehammer approach” that harms Virginia communities.

“Not only is the Fairwinds Landing project set up to support Virginia’s nation-leading offshore wind industry, but also to create hundreds of new jobs and bring economic growth to Hampton Roads and communities across Coastal Virginia,” Spanberger said in a statement. “Virginia deserves a governor whose focus is on delivering for our communities, not kowtowing to a President who is willfully unraveling our Commonwealth’s economy.”

Spanberger added that the administration also attempted to rescind $20 million for upgrades at the Portsmouth Marine Terminal, but the Port of Virginia had already completed that project.

BACKGROUND

Earlier this year — including at stops across Hampton Roads, Spanberger rolled out her plan to lower costs for Virginians across the board — her Affordable Virginia Plan, which focuses on lowering energy, healthcare, and housing costs for Virginia families.

This summer, Spanberger announced her Growing Virginia Plan in Suffolk to strengthen Virginia’s economy and expand opportunity across the Commonwealth.

And last month in Portsmouth, Spanberger  announced her Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan on day one to address challenges facing Virginia’s schools.

During her time in the U.S. House, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.
