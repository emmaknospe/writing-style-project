Spanberger: “Are You Ready to Have a Governor Who Puts the People of Virginia First?”

Buttigieg: “Abigail Spanberger Is Out There Every Day Talking About How to Lower the Cost of Healthcare, Lower the Cost of Housing, Lower the Cost of Utilities”

CHARLOTTESVILLE, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger last night rallied with supporters at the historic Jefferson Theater in Charlottesville — two weeks ahead of Election Day in Virginia — to encourage Virginians to cast their ballot and lay out the stakes in this year’s elections. Spanberger was joined by former U.S. Secretary of Transportation Pete Buttigieg.

In front of a crowd of more than 1,300 Virginians, Spanberger outlined the decisive action she will take as Governor to lower costs for Virginia families across the board.

“We know the stakes of this election and we know what we are for. We are for a Governor focused relentlessly on lowering costs — lowering costs in housing, healthcare, and energy, recognizing that there are a lot of challenges facing people in every corner of Virginia,” said Spanberger. “We need a Governor who has a plan in working with the General Assembly and their administration to drive down costs and make life more livable for Virginians.”

Spanberger continued, “Are you ready to make Virginia more affordable for all Virginians? Are you ready to have a Governor who puts the people of Virginia first and works every day to serve the people? One last one — are you ready to win?”

She was joined by Secretary Buttigieg, who highlighted the stakes in this year’s elections and encouraged Virginia voters to cast their ballots for Spanberger.

“With early voting underway even as we speak, Virginians can not only elect the leaders that you deserve, but also send a powerful message about what we all expect from our leaders. That is a message that will be heard around this country. Choosing a leader like Abigail Spanberger is not just a smart hire, it is a demand that policies change and a recognition that personnel is policy,” said Buttigieg. “Abigail Spanberger is out there every day talking about how to lower the cost of healthcare, lower the cost of housing, lower the cost of utilities — so that everyone cannot just get by, but get ahead and live well.”

Buttigieg continued, “In Abigail Spanberger, we have somebody who was literally named the most bipartisan Member of the whole delegation because what she cares about is just getting good things done. […] On issue after issue, she represents what most Virginians want to see — and her opponent absolutely does not.”

Spanberger spoke about the example set by her parents that fostered her deep commitment to serving her community and her country.

“I grew up in a house of service. My dad, an Army veteran and then a federal agent, my mother a nurse. And they always instilled upon us the idea that there’s no higher calling than service to country. It’s a message that I internalized. It’s a message that I believed in,” said Spanberger. “I grew up knowing that I wanted to follow in my dad’s footsteps, and I did. First as a federal agent and then as a CIA officer.”

Additionally, Spanberger called out her opponent for backing President Trump’s mass firings of federal workers — and made clear that will always stand up for Virginians and work to grow the Commonwealth’s economy.

“We have leaders in Richmond who refuse to recognize the impacts of bad legislation coming out of Washington that will raise prices for Virginians, of bad tariff policies that are hurting Virginians, and an attack on the federal workforce with DOGE 1.0 — and now their effort to continue it during a government shutdown,” said Spanberger. “These are attacks on Virginians and on our economy, and we need a Governor who will stand up against them.”

Spanberger continued, “We need a Governor who’s going to work to grow our economy, bring investments to the Commonwealth, [and] ensure that those with a great idea or a dream are able to start that business here.”

Spanberger also highlighted her focus on addressing the challenges facing Virginia students and working with parents and teachers to make sure Virginia schools are the best in the nation.

“We know that we want a Governor who will strengthen Virginia’s public schools — recognizing that the strength of our schools are the strength of our future, the strength of our workforce, the strength of our economy, and the way that we achieve and allow our kids to achieve every dream that might be theirs,” said Spanberger. “It is not just K-12 schools that matter, that are part of ensuring a pathway towards a bright future for all kids and for Virginians. It is our universities and our community college systems.”

Prior to Tuesday night’s event, Spanberger announced that she will finish out her campaign traveling across all 11 of Virginia’s congressional districts on the 11-day, 40-plus-stop “Virginia Votes Bus Tour.”

BACKGROUND

Spanberger remains focused on putting Virginians first as Governor — by lowering costs for Virginia families, growing Virginia’s economy, and making sure Virginia’s schools are the best in the nation.

Earlier this year, Spanberger released her Affordable Virginia Plan, which is focused on bringing down healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginians. The cost of living remains the top issue for Virginia voters.

In July, Spanberger rolled out her Growing Virginia Plan to grow workforce training opportunities for the next generation of Virginians, bring more business investment to Virginia, and expand trade opportunities for Virginia businesses. In the face of the Trump Administration’s attacks on Virginia’s economy and workforce — attacks that led the University of Virginia to forecast that the Commonwealth will have virtually no job growth in 2026, Spanberger will stand up for Virginia jobs.

In August, Spanberger announced her Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan to strengthen Virginia’s child care and early childhood education programs, K-12 schools, and institutions of higher education. As a mom of three kids in Virginia public schools, Spanberger has a plan to make sure Virginia’s schools are the best in America.
