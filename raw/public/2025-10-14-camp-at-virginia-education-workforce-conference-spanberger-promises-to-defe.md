Spanberger: “The Chaos Coming out of Washington Isn’t Just Bad Policy — It’s Creating Real Harm for Virginia Families and Businesses”

In 2020 and 2022, Spanberger Was Endorsed by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce for Her Support and Advocacy for Virginia’s Business Community

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger continues to stand up for Virginia’s workers and the Commonwealth’s economy — and today during an event hosted by business advocacy groups, including the Virginia Chamber of Commerce, she made clear how she will focus on strengthening Virginia public schools, investing in Virginia’s workforce, and protecting Virginians’ jobs as their next Governor.

While speaking at the 2025 Virginia Education & Workforce Conference, Spanberger shared the concerns she’s hearing from federal workers, small business owners, contractors, and business leaders across Virginia amid the Trump Administration’s mass layoffs of thousands of Virginia workers and continued attacks on Virginia’s economy.

“A few months ago, Virginia dropped from #1 to #4 in CNBC’s ‘Top State for Business’ rankings, and they were clear about one major factor: federal job cuts are disproportionately affecting Virginia’s economy. The chaos coming out of Washington isn’t just bad policy — it’s creating real harm for Virginia families and businesses,” said Spanberger during the event. “These aren’t just federal workers — they’re Virginians whose livelihoods depended on a stable federal workforce.”

Spanberger continued, “As Governor, I will refuse to let the chaos coming out of Washington define Virginia’s future. We need real plans to support displaced workers, attract new investment, and show businesses — and talent — that Virginia is a reliable place to build a career and a company. These are real challenges — the affordability crisis and the federal chaos — but I know that if we do the work and make the right investments, Virginia’s best days are still ahead.”

Spanberger also outlined her plans to strengthen Virginia’s economy by investing in public education — addressing the teacher shortage, increasing support for workforce training programs across the Commonwealth, and making sure every Virginia student has the resources and support they need to succeed.

“In K–12, we had nearly 3,000 teacher vacancies at the start of the 2024-2025 school year. And here’s what should alarm all of us: Virginia ranks 51st in the nation for post-pandemic math recovery. Dead last, behind every other state. We can and must do better,” said Spanberger. “I’ll work to recruit and retain teachers by increasing pay and expanding teacher mentorships, while investing in high-quality curriculum, dual enrollment classes, and career and technical education so students can earn industry credentials before graduation.”

Spanberger continued, “Together, we can build the strongest talent pipeline in America, continue to attract investment to our Commonwealth, and continue to grow the businesses that are here. We can make sure every student has a pathway to success. We can support displaced workers and attract new investment. We can get Virginia back to #1 — and keep it there.”

In 2020 and 2022, Spanberger was endorsed by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce for her support and advocacy for Virginia’s business community in the U.S. House — including for her role in moving the bipartisan infrastructure law over the finish line. And in June 2020, she received the U.S. Chamber of Commerce’s first-ever “Jefferson-Hamilton Award for Bipartisanship” for her commitment to finding common ground on issues facing Virginia’s small business community and overall economy.

BACKGROUND

In July, Spanberger announced her Growing Virginia Plan focused on protecting Virginia jobs and growing Virginia’s economy — including by creating job opportunities for Virginians through workforce training programs, increasing business investment in Virginia, and expanding trade opportunities for Virginia businesses and producers.

Spanberger has made clear that leaders should be focused on lowering costs, making it more affordable to live, and strengthening Virginia schools — not killing Virginia jobs and damaging Virginia’s economy.

On Saturday, Spanberger called on President Trump “to stop attacking Virginia’s workforce, to focus on bringing people together, and to put an end to this government shutdown” as Virginians already face the consequences of DOGE cuts, tariffs, and threats to healthcare coverage.

As President Trump is escalating his attacks on Virginia jobs, Winsome Earle-Sears — Virginia’s sitting Lieutenant Governor — continues to fail to stand up for Virginia’s families, workforce, and economy.

Last week — during the statewide gubernatorial debate at Norfolk State University, Winsome Earle-Sears outright refused to call on President Trump to stop this chaos when asked directly by the moderators. That refusal comes after Earle-Sears said DOGE cuts and attacks on Virginia’s economy were not a “real issue” in a CNN interview earlier this year.
