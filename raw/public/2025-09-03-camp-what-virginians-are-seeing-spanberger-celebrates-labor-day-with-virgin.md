Across the Commonwealth, Spanberger Shared Her Plans to Lower Virginians’ Costs, Grow Virginia’s Economy, and Strengthen Virginia Schools

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger this weekend joined Virginians across the Commonwealth to celebrate Labor Day and outline her vision for Virginia’s future as its next Governor.

Throughout the weekend, Spanberger listened to Virginians’ concerns and ideas for the future. She also shared her plans — her Affordable Virginia Plan, Growing Virginia Plan, and Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — to lower costs for Virginia workers and families, grow Virginia’s economy, and put the Commonwealth’s schools on a path toward being the best in America.

WHAT VIRGINIANS ARE SEEING

WDBJ [Roanoke]: Buena Vista Labor Day Parade kicks off election season

Spanberger, a former Virginia U.S. Representative, said the biggest concern she hears about in Southwest Virginia and across the state is affordability. She said she has a plan to address this.

“I have already rolled out an affordability agenda in housing, healthcare, and energy. I’ve rolled out a strengthening our schools plan for early childhood education, K-12, as well as for our universities and community colleges, and our growing Virginia’s Economy Plan focused on strengthening our workforce and bringing investment to Virginia,” said Spanberger, the Democratic candidate for governor.

Spanberger also talked about her plans to support rural healthcare in the state amid possible changes at the federal level following the passage of the Big Beautiful Bill.

“We know that per the bill that was signed into law, more than 300,000 Virginians will likely lose their healthcare. We know that six to seven rural hospitals will shut down. Because of some of the changes that they’ve made to the way funding mechanisms will go, over a course of 14 years, it’s a loss of $26 billion to Virginia hospitals,” she said. “The impact will be dire, but I’m looking at every opportunity that we have to strengthen the delivery of services through Medicaid, but also to work directly with our hospital systems to ensure that in any mechanism we possibly can create, we are ensuring we can provide care to people across communities.”

WTKR News 3 [Norfolk]: Abigail Spanberger rallies voters at Rep. Bobby Scott’s annual Labor Day cookout

Democratic candidate for Virginia governor Abigail Spanberger took to the stage to rally voters. As usual, hundreds of people attended the cookout.

The cookout also serves as the unofficial beginning of the statewide election campaign season.

“We are going to get to Richmond and roll out plans, in partnership with the General Assembly and through the Governor’s Office, to focus on lowering costs for Virginians,” said Spanberger.

“I have worked my entire time in politics building coalitions,” Spanberger said. “I may be a Democrat, but you do not have to be to vote for me.”

ABC 13 [Lynchburg]: Virginia democratic candidates kick off fall election season at Labor Day event

The small town setting is a critical stop on the campaign trail. Gubernatorial Candidate Abigail Spanberger and many other big-name democrats spent the day marching in a parade downtown, followed by a dueling rally at Glen Maury Park.

Spanberger emphasized her background in public service during her speech. “I grew up in a house of service. I began my career as a federal agent. I worked narcotics and money laundering cases, and from there I went to the CIA, where I served undercover my whole time focused on fighting terrorism,” Spanberger said.

She outlined her priorities, focusing on reducing the cost of living, housing, healthcare, and energy bills, and stressed the importance of bipartisan support. “Every corner of the Commonwealth, people talk about the cost of living, the cost of housing, the cost of health care, the cost of our energy bills, and I am running to work to lower costs,” she said.

Daily News-Record: Spanberger meets Shenandoah Valley Dems at Labor Day party

Festival Conference Center at James Madison University was packed Sunday evening as Abigail Spanberger, Democratic candidate for Virginia Governor, addressed Democrats from all over the Shenandoah Valley.

Hundreds of people cheered as Spanberger and her running mates, State Senator Ghazala Hashmi, D-Richmond, and Jay Jones, D-Norfolk, took the stage to explain their top priorities if they win the election in November.

Spanberger said that public education and affordable housing would be among her top priorities if she were elected Governor.

BACKGROUND

Earlier this year, Spanberger rolled out her plan to lower costs for Virginians — her Affordable Virginia Plan, which focuses on lowering healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

This summer, Spanberger shared her Growing Virginia Plan to strengthen Virginia’s economy and expand opportunity across the Commonwealth.

Spanberger has also announced her Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan on day one to address challenges facing Virginia’s schools.

As a member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was named the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.
