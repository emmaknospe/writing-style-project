Scott: “Abigail Spanberger is Going to Win in November 2025 — and Then, Together We are Going to Move Virginia Forward”

PORTSMOUTH, Va. —  Speaker of the Virginia House of Delegates Don Scott today endorsed U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger to serve as the next Governor of Virginia.

A prominent attorney and former U.S. Navy officer, Don Scott previously served as the House Democratic Leader, leading the Virginia House Democratic Caucus back into the majority in November 2023. In January 2024, he was elected Speaker of the Virginia House of Delegates — making history as the first Black Speaker elected in the body’s 400-year history.

“Last year, Virginians made history electing me as the first Black Speaker in our Commonwealth’s history. It’s time to make history again and elect Abigail Spanberger as our first woman Governor,” said Scott. “Abigail Spanberger doesn’t wait for others to take the first step — she acts decisively and with purpose. That’s the leadership we need in Richmond as we fight to deliver for everyday Virginians.”

“As Speaker of the Virginia House of Delegates, I am proud to endorse Abigail Spanberger to serve as the next Governor of Virginia,” continued Scott. “In Congress, she has delivered tremendous results for ALL Virginians. From investing in our infrastructure to addressing the addiction crisis in our communities, Congresswoman Spanberger has delivered landmark legislation. That’s why I trust her as Governor to work with the General Assembly to strengthen our public schools, create an economy that works for Virginia’s working families, and protect Virginians’ reproductive freedom. Abigail Spanberger is going to win in November 2025 — and then, together we are going to move Virginia forward.”

“Speaker Scott is dedicated to uplifting his fellow Virginians — and his ability to work with both parties shows his commitment to getting things done for the Commonwealth. I am truly grateful for his endorsement,” said Spanberger. “The people of Portsmouth are fortunate to have Don Scott as their delegate, and all Virginians are lucky to have Don Scott in their corner working tirelessly as the House Speaker. He has brought an extraordinary sense of determination, optimism, and focus to his work. Together, we will build a stronger future for the Virginia we all love.”
