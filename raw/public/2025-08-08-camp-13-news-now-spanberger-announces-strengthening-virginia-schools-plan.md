By Leila Cox

PORTSMOUTH, Va. — Abigail Spanberger announced her new Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan, which outlines her priorities for strengthening Virginia’s childcare and early childhood education programs, K-12 schools, and institutions of higher education.

Spanberger’s announcement comes as Virginia is facing a chronic teacher shortage and is lagging far behind the rest of the country in education recovery.

Virginia ranks 51st in the country, behind all other states and Washington, D.C., in math recovery from 2019 to 2024.

“As Virginia’s next governor, I will be focused on making sure Virginia is leading the way on academic excellence nationwide. I will support Virginia’s educators who go above and beyond to support our children. I will put Virginia students first,” said Spanberger.

Spanberger was joined by her running mates, State Senator Ghazala Hashmi and Delegate Jay Jones.

Virginia Senate President Pro Tempore L. Louise Lucas was also in attendance at Friday’s event, which was held at her alma mater, I.C. Norcom High School in Portsmouth.

“With Abigail Spanberger as governor, we will have a governor who puts Virginia families and students first. If we want stronger schools, she is what the community needs, and she is what our Commonwealth needs as well,” said Lucas.

Parents, students, educators and local leaders gathered in the I.C. Norcom High School library to listen to Spanberger’s vision for the Commonwealth’s public education system.

“We need to raise teachers’ salaries, and bring and attract people into the profession and ensure every educator has the respect they deserve for the incredible work they do,” said Spanberger.

As a parent and product of the Virginia public school system, Spanberger said she will work tirelessly to uphold academic excellence. In order to do so, she said improvement must start where students begin.

“Parents need access to quality early childhood care options that they can actually afford and that prepare our kids for kindergarten. As Virginia’s next governor, I will give parents more options and a little bit more breathing room,” said Spanberger.

A part of Spanberger’s Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan would establish greater access to the childcare subsidy program and create high-quality childcare in all communities.

She said no parent should have to choose between their career and enrolling their child into daycare or school.

Educational equity, teacher retention, equitable funding and workforce development are other key pillars in Spanberger’s extensive plan to support educators and students.

“The strength of Virginia’s schools is personal for me, just as it is to parents across the Commonwealth. As their children grow, parents deserve to know their child is receiving an outstanding education in our Commonwealth,” said Spanberger.
