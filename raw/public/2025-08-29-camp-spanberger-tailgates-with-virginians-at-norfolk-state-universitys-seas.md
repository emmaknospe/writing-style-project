**DOWNLOADABLE PHOTOS: Spanberger Tailgates with Virginians, Attends Norfolk State University’s Football Season Opener**

NORFOLK, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger yesterday tailgated with excited Virginians ahead of Norfolk State University’s football season opener and cheered on the Spartans from inside Dick Price Stadium.

Before kickoff, Spanberger met with Norfolk State students and alumni, local leaders, and Hampton Roads residents, listened to the issues that matter most to them, and shared in their excitement for the Spartan’s season.

“We spent a beautiful night together celebrating with new and returning students, avid fans, and families made up of generations of proud Norfolk State alumni. These Virginians want to see our next Governor get to work to address the challenges their families are facing and build a stronger future,” said Spanberger. “I’ve appreciated every one of our neighbors who has taken the time to share their vision with me — whether at a local business, their place of worship, or amid the excitement of a tailgate. While last night’s result wasn’t what fans wanted, I’m wishing the Spartans the best of luck the rest of this historic season.”

Spanberger was joined by Democratic Attorney General nominee Jay Jones, Delegate Cliff Hayes (HD-91), Delegate Bonita Anthony (HD-92), Delegate Alex Askew (HD-95), Michael Feggans (HD-97), and Virgil Thornton, candidate for House District 86.
