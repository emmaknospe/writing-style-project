Spanberger’s “Affordable Virginia Plan” Is Focused on Lowering Virginians’ Housing, Healthcare, & Energy Costs

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger’s focus on saving Virginians money — specifically, her Affordable Virginia Plan — continues to echo the top-of-mind concerns she is hearing from Virginia families.

New polling highlighted by The Hill shows the “rising cost of living is the most pressing issue” for Virginia voters. While Spanberger has laid out her priorities to lower costs for Virginia families, Winsome Earle-Sears has no substantive plan to respond to the pocketbook challenges facing the Commonwealth’s families. Instead, Sears has backed the Trump Administration’s attacks on Virginia workers, reckless tariff policy, and Republican tax law that are driving up costs, taking away healthcare coverage, and threatening Virginia’s economy.

The Hill: Rising cost of living top issue in Virginia ahead of vote for governor

The rising cost of living is the most pressing issue for nearly a third of Virginia voters as they cast their ballots in the upcoming state elections, which will decide a new governor, a new poll found.

The Virginia Commonwealth University (VCU) poll at the end of August found that 28 percent of registered voters said cost of living is their top issue, followed by reproductive rights (13 percent) and immigration and education (12 percent each).

—

BACKGROUND

Earlier this year, Spanberger rolled out her plan to lower costs for Virginians across the board — her Affordable Virginia Plan, which focuses on lowering energy, healthcare, and housing costs for Virginia families.

Last month, the Spanberger campaign released a new TV and digital ad, titled “Raise Costs,” highlighting Sears’ praise for the Republican bill signed into law last month by President Trump. Sears said the legislation — which is projected to raise Virginians’ healthcare costs, mortgage payments, and utility bills — “does so many great things.”

Spanberger has also announced her Growing Virginia Plan and Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan on day one to address challenges facing Virginia’s economy and schools.

As a Member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was named the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.
