By Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger

In less than 100 days, Virginians will choose our next governor. Voters will have the chance to decide the direction we take as a commonwealth – led by a Governor who will stand up for Virginia, or one who will continue to pay lip service to a president hellbent on setting Virginia back.

I’ve always supported smart, responsible efforts to cut fraud, waste and abuse from our government – but the ironically-named Department of Government Efficiency is neither smart nor responsible.

When President Trump launched DOGE, I quickly heard from career civil servants, air traffic controllers, Veterans Administration nurses, federal law enforcement officers and so many more Virginians who feared for their jobs. Federal firings are eliminating decades of expertise cultivated by Virginians who’ve chosen careers of service to their country and fellow Americans. Cuts and canceled contracts are making it harder for seniors, veterans and families across our commonwealth to receive support they’ve earned.

Months into this administration sledgehammering Virginia’s federal workforce and threatening our economy, the wide-ranging damage is cruel and undeniable. Virginia is the only state in the entire country that saw a statistically significant rise in unemployment this June. And last month, Virginia lost our number-one ranking as “America’s Top State for Business.” CNBC pointed to one factor in particular for the drop: the Trump Administration’s indiscriminate firing of Virginia workers.

Despite all of the data telling us that Virginia is moving in the wrong direction, our governor and lieutenant governor continue to let this chaos go unchecked. The governor’s office recently blamed Fairfax County leaders, not the Trump Administration, for the devastating local fallout from DOGE – all to avoid standing up to the man in the White House.

Not to be outdone, Virginia’s current lieutenant governor, my opponent in this year’s race for governor, said she doesn’t understand why people are upset about losing their jobs and that it “happens to everybody all the time.” That’s not leadership – that’s turning your back on Virginians.

We need a new approach that puts Virginia’s economy and workers first. I’m running for Governor with a real plan to get Virginia back on track.

I recently announced my “Growing Virginia Plan.” My plan focuses on three pillars: growing our world-class workforce, increasing business investment in our commonwealth and expanding trade opportunities for Virginia businesses and producers. Because with the right leadership, I know we can both contend with threats to our commonwealth and create an economy that expands opportunity for every Virginian.

And while federal cuts have an outsized impact on Virginia, DOGE isn’t the only threat to Virginia’s economy coming out of Washington. The Trump Administration’s unpredictable tariffs are raising prices on everyday goods, threatening Virginia’s largest industries and making it impossible for businesses to plan for the future.

The impact of these new taxes also underscores the integral role of trade in Virginia’s economic growth. Instead of undermining our economy, I will work to strengthen our trade relationships, leverage Virginia’s assets – such as Dulles International Airport – and find new markets for Virginia exports.

Virginia also can do more to prepare our workforce to meet the needs of today’s job market and tomorrow’s opportunities. Come January, my administration will focus on promoting workforce training and apprenticeship programs that set Virginians up for high-demand, good-paying careers. We will work with Virginia’s incredible K-12 schools, community colleges and four-year universities to provide students with meaningful, career-ready experiences. Not only do these programs benefit both business and labor, but they also build a foundation for long-term economic growth.

But we can’t prepare the next generation of Virginians for 21st-century jobs without building a strong business environment that attracts new investment. That begins with making sure Virginia has reliable infrastructure, housing and healthcare that families can afford, and nation-leading, strong public schools.

As governor, I will work aggressively to promote Virginia as the best state in the country to do business – bringing entrepreneurs, education and industry together to leverage the strengths and address the challenges facing each of Virginia’s unique regions.

I don’t care which political party you belong to – if you care about protecting Virginia’s economy, I want to talk.

If we don’t change course, Virginia is on a glide path toward losing its competitive edge. Our commonwealth needs a governor who is realistic about the threats we face and laser-focused on finding real solutions.

Statewide leaders who are cheering on the very policies that got us here and trying to make themselves feel better about job losses that happen under their watch won’t cut it.
