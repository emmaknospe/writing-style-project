By Sarah Allen

CHARLOTTESVILLE, Va. (CBS19 NEWS) — In what is being called her biggest turnout on the bus tour yet, Democratic nominee for governor Abigail Spanberger stopped in Charlottesville.

“We need a governor who will work tirelessly to not just grandstand and talk about problems but to actually endeavor to govern,” Spanberger said.

Spanberger, who was accompanied by Democratic candidate for lieutenant governor Ghazala Hashmi and Democratic attorney general candidate Jay Jones, timed her stop with the third anniversary of the overturning of Roe v. Wade. She said if elected governor, Virginia will continue to be the only state in the South that allows a woman access to an abortion.

“Legislation to protect access to contraception, had that bill come across my desk, unlike our current governor, I would have signed it,” Spanberger said.

And the issue is a personal one to Hashmi, who as a state Senator led the Right to Contraception Act through the General Assembly.

“I experienced what so many women do experience, loss of pregnancies. I lost twins, and my body did not know how to expel them,” Hashmi said.

Hashmi said from her experience, she wants women to have the freedom she did at the time, prior to the Dobbs decision.

“We shouldn’t have to ask the permission of politicians to be making personal decisions for our lives and our families,” Hashmi said.

Spanberger also addressed one of her key points on her platform, making Virginia more affordable.

“There is a need for prioritization from the governor’s office in how as a state we prioritize increasing supply,” Spanberger said.

Spanberger says that the threat of the federal bill to cut Medicaid programs to healthcare is one that will affect thousands of Virginians.

“If that were to be signed into law, we would see hundreds of thousands of Virginians who would lose their access to health care. With that, the estimates are that at least six of our rural hospitals would be so devastated they may close,” Spanberger said.

Tuesday’s stop is one of 40 on the eight-day bus tour, kicking off the race to the governor mansion that is just months away. Spanberger’s opponent in the race is Republican Winsome Earle Sears.
