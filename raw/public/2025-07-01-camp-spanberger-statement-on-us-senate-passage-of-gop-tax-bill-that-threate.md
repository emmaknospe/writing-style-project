Spanberger: “The Legislation Passed in the U.S. Senate Today would Rip away Healthcare Coverage from Hundreds of Thousands of Our Neighbors”

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today released the following statement following passage of Congressional Republicans’ tax bill — legislation that threatens to take away healthcare coverage from more than 300,000 Virginians.

“In every corner of our Commonwealth, I hear from Virginians who are concerned about rising healthcare costs. At this moment, they deserve elected officials who will defend access to affordable coverage and work every day to lower healthcare and prescription drug costs. Instead, Virginians are watching as Republicans in Washington inject chaos into our healthcare system.

“The legislation passed in the U.S. Senate today would rip away healthcare coverage from hundreds of thousands of our neighbors, including Virginians with disabilities. Additionally, this bill would threaten the survival of our rural hospitals, overwhelm our emergency rooms as more Virginians forego preventative care and check-ups, and drive up costs for Virginia families. This misguided bill is a direct threat to the stability of our Commonwealth’s healthcare system and the financial security of Virginia parents, families, and seniors.

“Virginians deserve a Governor who will speak out against these attacks on Medicaid and the Affordable Care Act — not hide in silence. As our next Governor, I will never be afraid to stand up for the health, safety, and prosperity of the people of Virginia.”
