Governor-Elect Spanberger: “I Will Always Promote Virginia as the Best State in America for Business”

VIRGINIA BEACH, Va. — Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger today joined the Hampton Roads Chamber of Commerce at its annual meeting to outline her incoming administration’s focus on strengthening Virginia’s economy for both workers and businesses — including by lowering costs for Virginia families, attracting new investment to the Commonwealth, and bringing Virginia back to its status as the “Top State for Business in the Country.”

“I will do everything in my power to respond to uncertainty by maintaining a stable and predictable business environment. And I will always promote Virginia as the best state in America for business,” said Spanberger. “Over the next four years, I will be laser-focused on attracting new business investment to our Commonwealth, expanding trade opportunities for Virginia businesses, and building an economy where every Virginian can earn a good living, support their family, and have confidence in a secure retirement. Because that’s what our fellow Virginians deserve.”

Throughout her remarks, Spanberger highlighted her administration’s plans to grow Virginia’s economy by investing in Virginia’s workforce to make sure workers have the skills and education they need to succeed in the jobs of today — and power the economy of tomorrow.

“As Governor, my administration will work with Virginia’s K-12 schools, community colleges, and four-year institutions to promote career and technical education programs,” said Spanberger. “We will provide students with meaningful career-ready experiences — and investments in internships and apprenticeships will be an essential part of this effort. We need to make sure these opportunities lead to good-paying jobs here in Virginia.”

“My administration will also focus on opportunities for reskilling, so Virginians are able to meet the demands of a rapidly changing economy,” Spanberger continued. “This includes supporting Virginia’s Veterans as they transition back into civilian life, and helping them fully leverage the invaluable skills, expertise, and leadership they’ve developed and honed during their service to our country.”

Spanberger also reaffirmed her commitment to making Virginia more affordable for families in Hamptons Road and across the Commonwealth.

“I hear from families in every corner of our Commonwealth who are concerned about costs and want to be confident they can continue to call Virginia home, raise their children here, and continue to support their families without going into massive debt,” said Spanberger. “I made affordability the central pillar of my campaign platform — and it will be a top focus of my work with the General Assembly come January.”

Spanberger continued, “This concern isn’t limited to families. It’s a concern I hear from business executives and employers who know that the strength of Virginia’s economy depends on the quality of our workforce and our ability to outcompete 49 other states and the District of Columbia.”
