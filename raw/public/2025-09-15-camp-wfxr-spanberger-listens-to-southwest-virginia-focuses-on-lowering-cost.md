WFXR: “Spanberger Has Kept a Constant Ear Bent to the Concerns of Voters in the Area”

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger continues to highlight her plans to lower Virginians’ energy bills, rent and mortgage payments, and healthcare costs as their next Governor.

New reporting from WFXR News in Roanoke underscores Spanberger’s continued commitment to listening to Virginians across Southwest Virginia and laying out plans — including her Affordable Virginia Plan focused on lowering costs across the board — to address their top-of-mind worries.

WFXR [Roanoke]: Virginia gubernatorial candidates address concerns over rising costs as early voting nears

The southwestern corner of the state has long been a Republican stronghold, but Democrats have made up ground in some localities in recent elections, and Spanberger has kept a constant ear bent to the concerns of voters in the area.

“The number one concern Abigail hears from Republicans and Democrats in Southwest Virginia, the Roanoke Valley, and across the Commonwealth is the squeeze of high costs,” said a spokesperson with the Spanberger campaign on Saturday.

[…]

The Spanberger campaign pointed to the former U.S. Congresswoman’s Affordable Virginia Plan, which was unveiled in May.

The plan seeks to lower energy, housing, and healthcare costs statewide and outlines a strategy to make Virginia more energy independent by aiming in part to increase local power generation sources with low or no fuel cost and maximize the use of energy efficiency measures.

“Abigail has rolled out real plans to lower energy costs for all Virginians,” said a spokesperson for the Spanberger campaign on Sunday. “As Virginia’s next Governor, Abigail will work across the aisle to lower costs for consumers, drive economic growth, and put the Commonwealth on a path toward greater energy independence.”

BACKGROUND

This year, Spanberger has already announced her Affordable Virginia Plan, Growing Virginia Plan, and Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan day one to address challenges in Virginia’s cost of living, economy, and schools.

As a Member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was named the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.
