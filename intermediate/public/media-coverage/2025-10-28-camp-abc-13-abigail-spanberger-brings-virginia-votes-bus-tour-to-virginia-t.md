By Hayden Robertson

BLACKSBURG, Va. (WSET) — With just a week left until Election Day, Virginia Democrat Gubernatorial candidate Abigail Spanberger brought her final push bus tour to Virginia Tech to speak to students and members of the community.

In the final days before November 4, Spanberger and her team launched the “Virginia Votes” bus tour to speak to every voter across the Commonwealth before Election Day and reminding them why voting is extremely important. Her tour made a stop Monday night at Virginia Tech to speak with students and members of the surrounding communities.

Spanberger was welcomed on stage by the Blacksburg Democrat Delegate candidate Lily Franklin. During her nearly 20-minute speech to the over 100 people who attended, Spanberger was confident and said she’s excited to usher in a new chapter for Virginia.

“I’m thrilled to be running for governor,” Spanberger said. “And I am excited because in a week and some hours, we will win!”

Spanberger also addressed several issues that she said are impacting Virginians, like a loss of healthcare and support for education. She aimed at the current government shutdown and the numerous tariffs that President Donald Trump has signed, causing price increases for families.

“We need a governor who is going to stand up for Virginianians and push back against every attack on our Commonwealth that is coming from this Congress or out of this White House,” Spanberger said.

Many voters in the crowd resonated with her message and are urging every Virginian to get out and vote so their voices can be heard.

“Abigail said it best; she said that everything is politics and politics is everything,” Virginia Tech junior Mason Harris said. “And so I say whatever issues you care about, whatever thing you care about, it does involve politics.”

“I would say if you’re gonna complain about all these policies, you gotta do something about it,” Virginia Tech senior Elliott Newman said. “The people are the strongest thing in the country, the Constitution says we the people.”

Spanberger and her team headed to Northern Virginia after finishing at Virginia Tech for the next start of the tour.

ABC13 will check in with her on Tuesday when she makes a stop in Nelson County.
