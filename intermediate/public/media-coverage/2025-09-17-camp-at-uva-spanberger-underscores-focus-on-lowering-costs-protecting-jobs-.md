29News: “Spanberger Says She’s All About Finding Creative Solutions to Healthcare, Affordability, and Housing Problems”

CHARLOTTESVILLE, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger — an alumna of the University of Virginia — last night sat down in the Rotunda Dome Room on Grounds with Dr. Larry Sabato, founder and director of the UVA Center for Politics. During the conversation, Spanberger emphasized her plans to lower costs for Virginians, protect Virginia’s economy, and strengthen Virginia’s public schools as the next Governor of the Commonwealth.

Spanberger spoke about how the Republican tax law is threatening hundreds of thousands of Virginians’ healthcare coverage, as well as her focus on contending with the cuts in this law and working to protect Virginians’ access to care.

“The bill will increase costs in housing, in energy, and in healthcare — and in healthcare specifically, it will be nearly catastrophic for Virginia. More than 350,000 Virginians are likely to lose their healthcare. We know that at least six rural hospitals are likely to close. Our safety net hospitals are going to be facing dire circumstances and decisions. Already, the state Medicaid system has said that they’re expecting $26 billion less coming into the state. That is an impact that you cannot just make up.”

“I’m of the opinion that you can really only contend with or deal with problems that you ultimately can admit. The first bit of recognizing how bad it may be is understanding that, and from there you can actually proceed with trying to mitigate the harm.”

“In order to mitigate harm through the Governor’s Office and through the proper administration at the state level, we have to do all that we can possibly do to create as best a scaffolding as possible — so as few people as possible will lose their healthcare.”

Spanberger also underscored the outsized impact of the Trump Administration’s DOGE cuts on Virginia — and how as Virginia’s next Governor, she will stand up for Virginians’ jobs and Virginia’s economy.

“I do think it’s incredibly important that we actually have a Governor who will stand up for our economy and the Virginians who’ve lost jobs — and push back against a reckless administration that is wreaking havoc on our economy,”

Additionally, Spanberger made clear her commitment to strengthening Virginia’s public schools and setting students up for success in the classroom.

“Public schools are central to the strength of our communities, our civic engagement, and the future of our workforce and the next generation. Investing in schools, paying teachers, and ensuring that our kids have strong school buildings surrounding them — particularly in many communities where some of our schools are close to, if not even, 100 years old — is a major priority.”

Spanberger says she’s proud of her bipartisan track record, but still stands firm in what she believes in.

“I think that it’s important to recognize there has to be a level of intentionality about trying to find common ground.”

One way she wants to be intentional is in bringing change to Virginia, particularly to its economy.

“The reality is that the impact of the tariffs have been significant on Virginia … but importantly, a lot of priorities that I intend to pursue, not all of those elements of the plan cost money.”

Spanberger says she’s all about finding creative solutions to healthcare, affordability, and housing problems. Some things she supports spending on, like improving public school infrastructure across the state.

“Those types of investments, yes, those do cost actual dollars, but I think it’s a question of what are the investments that we’re making.”

BACKGROUND

Earlier this year, Spanberger rolled out her plan to lower costs for Virginians across the board — her Affordable Virginia Plan, which focuses on lowering energy, healthcare, and housing costs for Virginia families.

Last month, the Spanberger campaign released a new TV and digital ad, titled “Raise Costs,” highlighting Sears’ praise for the Republican bill signed into law last month by President Trump. Sears said the legislation — which is projected to raise Virginians’ healthcare costs, mortgage payments, and utility bills — “does so many great things.”

Spanberger has also announced her Growing Virginia Plan and Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan on day one to address challenges facing Virginia’s economy and schools.

As a Member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was named the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.
