Spanberger: “The Trump Administration’s Sledgehammer Approach to Governing is Hurting Virginia”

RICHMOND, Va. — As reported on the front page of Friday’s Virginian-Pilot, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger is calling out the Trump Administration for jeopardizing hundreds of Virginia jobs in Hampton Roads as new data shows U.S. job growth sharply declined in August.

According to the Pilot, the Trump Administration cancelled a “$39 million grant for Fairwinds Landing, a more-than $500 million maritime facility in Norfolk’s Lamberts Point connected to shipbuilding, ship repair and offshore wind.” The project was initially “expected to create 750 jobs” for the region.

Virginian-Pilot: Two Hampton Roads wind energy grants were canceled. But $20M was already spent.

—

This week, Democratic candidate for governor Abigail Spanberger released a statement also criticizing the withdrawal of funding.

“The Trump administration’s sledgehammer approach to governing is hurting Virginia — from firing thousands of federal workers to slashing federal funds for critical projects across our Commonwealth,” Spanberger said.

Spanberger’s opponent in the race, Lt. Gov. Winsome Earle-Sears, did not respond to a request for comment.

—

While Winsome Earle-Sears has been silent on the Trump Administration’s decision to withdraw this critical funding and continues to back President Trump’s job-killing policies, Spanberger is talking every day about her plans to lower costs for Virginia families, grow Virginia’s economy, and protect Virginians’ jobs.

BACKGROUND

Earlier this year — including at stops across Hampton Roads, Spanberger rolled out her plan to lower costs for Virginians across the board — her Affordable Virginia Plan, which focuses on lowering energy, healthcare, and housing costs for Virginia families.

This summer, Spanberger announced her Growing Virginia Plan in Suffolk to strengthen Virginia’s economy and expand opportunity across the Commonwealth.

And last month in Portsmouth, Spanberger  announced her Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan on day one to address challenges facing Virginia’s schools.

During her time in the U.S. House, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.
