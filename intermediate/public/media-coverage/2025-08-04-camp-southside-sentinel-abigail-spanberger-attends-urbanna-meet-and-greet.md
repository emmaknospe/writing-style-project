By Staff

Former Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger on Tuesday, July 22 joined community members from across Middlesex County to share her plans to build a stronger future for Virginia, listen to Virginians’ top-of-mind challenges, and celebrate the values that make the commonwealth strong.

In front of a packed room in Urbanna, Spanberger outlined her vision for the commonwealth — one that will allow more families to put down roots in Virginia by lowering costs, strengthening public schools, and putting the needs of Virginians above chaos and partisan politics.

“I’m grateful to all the Virginians I met in Urbanna for welcoming me into their community, sharing their deep-rooted pride in this beautiful region, and reaffirming our shared commitment to building a brighter future for every Virginia family,” said Spanberger. “As the next governor of Virginia, I will always make sure the interests of Coastal Virginia are represented in Richmond — and I will always work to lower costs, for Virginians, make Virginia’s schools the best in America, and stand up for the values that make this community strong.”

Earlier this year, Spanberger announced her Affordable Virginia Plan to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families. Across the commonwealth, Spanberger hears from Virginians who are struggling to keep up with rising costs — and as the next governor of Virginia, she is ready to get to work to make Virginia more affordable and save Virginians money.

And just last week, Spanberger announced her Growing Virginia Plan to grow workforce training programs, business investments, and trade opportunities across the commonwealth.

As a three-term member of Congress, representing the 7th District in central and northern Virginia, Spanberger made it her priority to lower costs for Virginia families, protect the commonwealth’s economy, and stand up for the interests of Coastal Virginia. Spanberger decided not to seek a fourth term in 2025 so she could run for governor. She is facing incumbent Lt. Gov. and Republican nominee for governor Winsome Earle-Sears in the Nov. 4 election.

Spanberger, a Democrat, built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to get things done for Virginia — including championing policies to support Virginia’s rural communities as the only Virginian on the U.S. House Agriculture Committee.
