By Heather Olinger

RICHMOND, Va. (WVVA) – Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger addressed the Virginia Chamber of Commerce today during the presentation of Blueprint Virginia 2035, a strategic plan aimed at strengthening the Commonwealth’s economy over the next decade. Spanberger framed the blueprint as a guide for her incoming administration and for collaboration across state and local government, the private sector, and communities.

This blueprint provides a roadmap for how we strengthen the Commonwealth’s reputation as the best place to live, work and raise a family.

This document will help us seize on the strengths of our workforce, our infrastructure and our business environment. This blueprint charts a course for how my administration, both parties in the General Assembly, our federal and local partners and everyone across our Virginia communities can work together to foster economic prosperity. To everyone who was involved in this monumental effort, thank you. – Virginia Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger

Spanberger said her administration will focus on lowering the cost of living for Virginians while boosting economic competitiveness and retaining top talent. She emphasized policies that would address barriers that keep people from working, including health care costs, childcare and housing availability.

I think everyone in the room can appreciate how lowering costs for everyone is essential to the success of our economy.

It isn’t just a family budget issue — it’s an economic competitiveness issue. – Virginia Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger

She framed workforce retention as central to the Commonwealth’s future growth.

We certainly can’t accept the status quo when it comes to the cost of living. When people want to work, they should be able to work. They shouldn’t be forced to wait in the wings because of the cost of health care, the lack of childcare or their housing situation.

These factors can slow down Virginia’s ability to grow and our ability to dominate. – Virginia Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger

Spanberger also said she will seek policies to bolster small business, entrepreneurship and innovation, and to make state programs more competitive and effective.

Over the next four years, I intend to advance policies that invest and reinvest in Virginia. I intend to retool our programs to make Virginia’s communities even more competitive.

I want to ensure that we are renewing our commitment to small businesses, entrepreneurship and innovation. And as I’ve said, I intend to work every single day to ensure Virginia remains a leader in the 21st century — and ultimately the number-one state for business again. – Virginia Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger

Blueprint Virginia 2035, developed with input from business leaders and policymakers, outlines priorities such as workforce development, infrastructure investment, regional competitiveness and support for targeted industries. Spanberger’s remarks positioned the plan as a shared agenda that her administration will use to guide policy choices and partnerships with the General Assembly and local governments.
