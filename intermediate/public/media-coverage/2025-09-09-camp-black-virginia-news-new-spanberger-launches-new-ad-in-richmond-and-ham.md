By Staff

A new campaign ad released today by former Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger takes aim at Lt. Gov. Winsome Earle-Sears, criticizing her support for a Republican health care law signed last month by President Donald J. Trump.

The ad, titled The Pain, highlights Trump’s budget that could strip health coverage from more than 350,000 Virginians who rely on Medicaid, the Affordable Care Act and Medicare.

The new Spanberger ad:

Economists have also warned that Virginia is on track for virtually no job growth in 2026, a forecast they attribute to the White House’s broader economic agenda. The budget contains billions for detention centers and ICE related funding.

Sears has praised the Trump budget, calling it “a great law that does so many great things.” Spanberger’s ad will air across the Norfolk and Richmond media markets, two areas with many Black voters — a key demographic for Democratic statewide candidates.
