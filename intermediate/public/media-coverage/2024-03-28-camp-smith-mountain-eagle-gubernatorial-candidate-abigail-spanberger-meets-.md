By Lauren Hafner, SML Democrats

Submitted in a news release to Smith Mountain Eagle by Lauren Hafner of SML Democrats:

Abigail Spanberger, candidate for Governor of Virginia in 2025, met with local Democrats at the SML Democrats office in Moneta on March 17th.

Ms. Spanberger considers all questions from constituents to be valuable as “there are no partisan questions.”  She finds ones that begin with “I didn’t vote for you, but …” to be one of the best compliments she receives.

As she did in Congress, Ms Spanberger pledges to work as Governor to invest in people, jobs and benefits for all people of Virginia.  Pictured from left: Don Vineyard, Chair Franklin County Democratic Committee; Diane Wallace, President SML Democrats; Spanberger; Karen Nuzzo, Chair Bedford Democratic Committee; and Doug Stevens, Chair Election 2024 Committee of SML Democrats.
