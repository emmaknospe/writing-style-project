By Michael Martz

emocrat Abigail Spanberger will launch a statewide bus tour in her race for governor in Petersburg on Saturday, ending 11 days later at her election night party in the Richmond area, where she lives.

Spanberger, who led an eight-day, 49-stop bus tour across Virginia in June, plans to make more than 40 stops in a final push in her race against Republican Lt. Gov. Winsome Earle-Sears to become the first woman elected governor of Virginia.

The former congresswoman said she will continue to focus on lowering everyday household costs as the central theme of her campaign, as she tries to portray Earle-Sears as more loyal to President Donald Trump than to Virginians.

Spanberger’s new bus tour tour will make stops in all 11 congressional districts in Virginia, with multiple stops in some localities. The campaign will announce details of the planned stops later this week, but it will begin on Saturday in Petersburg, in the 4th Congressional District, before traveling to Hampton and Norfolk, both in the 3rd District.

On Sunday, Oct. 26, the tour plans stops in Portsmouth and Norfolk before moving to Franklin in Virginia’s Southside in the 2nd District. It will move to Southwest Virginia on Monday, Oct. 27, with stops in Lee County, Bristol, Floyd County and Blacksburg, all in the 9th District.

On Tuesday, Oct. 28, Spanberger will visit Winchester, Page County and Harrisonburg in the Shenandoah Valley, in the 6th District, before crossing the Blue Ridge Mountains for stops in Nelson and Albemarle counties in the 5th District.

The tour will move east again on Wednesday, Oct. 29, with stops in Essex County, in the 1st District; Newport News, in the 3rd District; Virginia Beach, Chesapeake and Suffolk, in the 2nd District.

Spanberger will move through Northern Virginia on Thursday, Oct. 30, beginning in Prince William County — part of which she represented in her third term in Congress for the 7th Congressional District. The tour also will make stops that day in Alexandria, Fairfax County, Manassas and Loudoun County, encompassing parts of the 8th, 11th, 7th and 10th districts.

She will return to the Richmond area on Halloween Friday, Oct. 31, with stops in Hanover County, Richmond, Petersburg and Chesterfield County. (The region includes parts of the 1st, 4th and 5th districts.)

The tour will move back to Norfolk on Saturday, Nov. 1, and then return to Northern Virginia on Sunday, Nov. 2, with stops in Prince William, Fairfax and Falls Church.

On Monday, Nov. 3, Spanberger will return to the Richmond area, with stops in Henrico, Petersburg and Richmond the day before the election.

The campaign has not released details of her plans on Election Day, Nov. 4, but she will arrive by tour bus that night for an election night party at an undisclosed location in the Richmond area.
