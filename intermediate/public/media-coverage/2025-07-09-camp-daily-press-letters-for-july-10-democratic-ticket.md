By Beverly Peterson

At a June 28 rally sponsored by Historic Triangle Democrats, I heard excellent speeches by the Democratic Party candidates for governor, lieutenant governor and attorney general — Abigail Spanberger, Ghazala Hashmi and Jay Jones — spelling out their reasons for wanting to serve Virginia.

I also met Grace, a 20-something volunteer who approached me with a clipboard and asked me if I’d be willing to write postcards, make calls or knock on doors to support the ticket. I had my excuses ready. Then I asked Grace why she was working so hard on this election. I did not expect the answer she gave.

She said her mother is a teacher who makes $45,000 a year and her father works for the Marines. She does not see herself as someone who will benefit from generational wealth. Instead, she fears she will never be able to afford to buy a house.

So, I will support this ticket that promises to do right by the middle class by making some calls and writing postcards. And Grace, I’m walking with you to knock on doors.
