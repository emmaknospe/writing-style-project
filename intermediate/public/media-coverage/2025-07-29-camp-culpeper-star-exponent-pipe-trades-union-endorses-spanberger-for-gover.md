By Staff

The Mid-Atlantic Pipe Trades Association — representing six locals of the United Association of Plumbers and Steamfitters across Virginia, Maryland, and the District of Columbia — last week endorsed Abigail Spanberger as the next Governor of Virginia.

“Abigail has a proven record of delivering results for Virginia’s working families. Virginians deserve a Governor who will put Virginia’s workers and economy first — not dismiss their concerns and downplay the economic challenges many Virginians currently face,” said Jason Ascher, Political Director of the Mid-Atlantic Pipe Trades Association.

“Our Local unions look forward to working with Abigail to expand the use of our registered apprenticeships, ensuring that Virginia has the best-trained workforce, fighting the plague of wage theft, and protecting good union construction jobs. All of this while promoting better working conditions and safer job sites for all workers in the Commonwealth.”

Spanberger in a statement said she is grateful for the support.

“Everywhere I travel across the Commonwealth, I hear from Virginians who work hard, play by the rules, and want security and prosperity for their families. But at this moment, so many Virginians are putting in longer hours, yet finding it even harder to make ends meet,” she said.

Spanberger said her recently announced Affordable Virginia Plan and Growing Virginia Plan aims to deliver better wages, strong investments in training and registered apprenticeship programs, fair treatment in the workplace, and a more affordable Virginia for working families.

“Together, we will put Virginia’s workers and economy first,” she said.

The Mid-Atlantic Pipe Trades Association was established in January 2019 with the merger or the Maryland Pipe Trades Association, Virginia Pipe Trades Association, and the Multi-states Pipe Trades Council. They are an affiliate of the United Association of Plumbers, Steamfitter, Welders, and HVAC Techs.
