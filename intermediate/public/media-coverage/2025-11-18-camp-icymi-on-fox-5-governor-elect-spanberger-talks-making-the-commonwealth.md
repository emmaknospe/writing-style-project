Governor-Elect Spanberger: “We Need to Constantly Be Looking at Places Where the Choices of My Future Administration Can Have a Positive Impact on the Goals of Lowering Costs or Increasing Affordability”

RICHMOND, Va. — Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger joined FOX 5’s “The Final 5” last night as she continues to make clear that her Administration will be relentlessly focused on lowering costs for Virginia families starting on day one.

Click here to watch Governor-elect Spanberger on FOX 5 and read excerpts below.

—

Much of what I talked about across the campaign trail was what I was hearing from voters — affordability, particularly in the places where you can feel it the most: healthcare, housing, your energy bill, for some families their day care.

And so my priority moving forward is to make sure that every lever we can pull. So that means bills that we’re going to push through the General Assembly to work to lower costs — we’re going to do it. But it also means having a whole of government — at the state level — approach, places where we can, through regulatory change, through gubernatorial action within our administration, take some of the stressors off of the things that are driving up costs.

—

My marching orders to the cabinet that I will ultimately select and leadership across my administration will be to constantly be looking at places where the choices of my future administration can have a positive impact on the goals of lowering costs or increasing affordability.

—

Ultimately, I don’t think there should be any surprises for anyone who may be watching that, as I am releasing that 100 Day Plan and endeavoring to deliver on it, that it is squarely focused on issues of affordability and trying to make an impact for Virginia families, small business owners, and our larger economy.

—

I know by the numbers that multiple counties that voted for President Trump in ’16, ’20 and ’24 then voted for me in 2025. And sometimes that’s talked about in just the political sense of what it means.

But for me, it means that people who multiple times over chose to put their faith and their trust in President Trump and perhaps always with Republicans or maybe specific to him, in 2025 elected to vote for me.

And what I see in that reality is that I have an enormous responsibility to be honest with people about the challenges ahead, to be relentless in pursuing policies that I know will positively impact them in their lives, their economy, their kids.

—
