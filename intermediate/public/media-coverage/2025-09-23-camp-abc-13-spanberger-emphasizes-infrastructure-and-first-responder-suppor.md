By Alexia Stanbridge

ROANOKE, Va. (WSET) — Democratic gubernatorial candidate Abigail Spanberger addressed truck drivers and firefighters in Roanoke, emphasizing her commitment to infrastructure and first responders.

Spanberger, who has a background in law enforcement, discussed her plans to invest in roads and bridges to ensure the safety of truckers.

Speaking to firefighters, she highlighted her legislative efforts, including advocating for bills like Social Security benefits for first responders. “I believe that you deserve leaders who will do everything in their power to connect you with the resources that you need to do your jobs and the benefits you have earned through your service,” Spanberger said.

“As a member of Congress, I worked alongside Virginia’s firefighters and first responders to make progress in your and our shared priorities, and as Virginia’s next governor, you can trust that I will continue doing the same,” she said.

Her campaign priorities include growing Virginia’s economy, improving education, enhancing affordability, and increasing housing options. The Virginia Professional Fire Fighters Union has endorsed Spanberger, and many union members attended the event.

Spanberger expressed gratitude for the support of both the firefighters’ union and the Police Benevolent Association, Virginia’s largest police union. “Well, I’m certainly grateful for their support,” she said. “I worked in partnership with the firefighters to get a bill passed at the federal level to repeal a long-term Social Security retirement benefits issue for firefighters, police, and other public servants.”

Spanberger reiterated her dedication to supporting first responders and stated that if elected, she would continue to prioritize them. She plans to make more visits to the area to engage with voters. The Virginia Professional Fire Fighters Association previously endorsed Terry McAuliffe in his 2021 campaign against Governor Glenn Youngkin.
