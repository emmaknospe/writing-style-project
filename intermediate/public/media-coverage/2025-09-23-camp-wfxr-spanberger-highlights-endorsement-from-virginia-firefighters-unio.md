By Paul Hess

Virginia’s democratic candidate for governor, Abigail Spanberger, delivered remarks on Tuesday, September 23, in Roanoke, highlighting her endorsement from the Virginia Professional Fire Fighters (VPFF).

The former Congresswoman was joined by Edward Kelly, president of the International Association of Fire Fighters, Robert Bragg, president of Virginia Professional Fire Fighters, plus dozens of local firefighters and first responders.

“The people of Virginia need Abigail to bring her acute political capabilities to the Statehouse to drive those bipartisan efforts that we need now,” Kelly said. “We don’t have time to bicker; we have to work together.”

The VPFF represents more than 10,000 firefighters, paramedics, and emergency response workers across the Commonwealth. The organization announced the endorsement back in July.

“We’re pleased to have endorsed Abigail for her service as a federal law enforcement officer, and her record of support for fire fighters and their families. Abigail led the repeal of the WEP/GPO program that has put thousands of dollars back in the hands of retired fire fighters,” said Robert L. Bragg III, President, Virginia Professional Fire Fighters. “For her commitment to continuing to support and fight for the rights of all public safety professionals across the Commonwealth, we stand beside Abigail and fully support her to become the 75th Governor of the great Commonwealth of Virginia.”

Spanberger mentioned the work she has done in the past, assisting firefighters and other first responders, highlighted by passing the Social Security Fairness Act.

According to Spanberger, the act has already delivered $310 million in earned retirement benefits and previously denied benefits from both the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) and the Government Pension Offset (GPO).

“We have a very interesting job; it’s not 9-5,” Bragg said. “We deal with a lot of stress and mental health issues, and she understands that.”

Spanberger said she wants to continue to support Virginia’s first responders if she’s elected Governor.

“I look forward as Governor to continuing to ensure that first responders and firefighters are heard, for the important work that they do and the incredible risks that they take on behalf of our communities,” she said. “I’m really appreciative that they’re doing this event.”

Between now and election night, Spanberger said she’s committed to traveling across the commonwealth, including Southwest, to let Virginians know she’s listening to their concerns, and how she can tie them into her priorities.

“Certainly with rising energy costs, particularly impacting far Southwest (Virginia),” Spanberger said. “The plan that I’ve put forth related to energy costs and bringing them down, I know, is particularly relevant.”

Spanberger also addressed members of the Virginia Trucking Association on Tuesday, during the final day of its annual convention, which was held at Hotel Roanoke & Conference Center.
