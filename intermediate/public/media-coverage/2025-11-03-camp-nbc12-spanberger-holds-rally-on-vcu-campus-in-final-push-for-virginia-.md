By Maggie Glass

RICHMOND, Va. (WWBT) – Democratic candidate for governor Abigail Spanberger made a campaign stop at Virginia Commonwealth University on Monday.

Congresswoman Jennifer McClellan and Michigan Senator Elissa Slotkin joined her.

Emerson College polling shows Spanberger with an 11-point lead over her opponent, Republican Winsome Earle-Sears.

Spanberger told the college students that if elected, she plans to help lower the cost of living while growing the state’s economy.

“In Virginia, we need a governor who will stand up against the chaos and endeavor to create stability and steadiness and job growth here on the ground,” Spanberger said.

The Democratic candidate also addressed voters who are unhappy about national politics under the Trump administration.

“I’m not going to be able to stop everything, every bad policy coming out of Washington, I’m going to be truthful about that. But we need a Governor who is working tirelessly to mitigate every harm that might be caused by a reckless and chaotic administration,” Spanberger said.

For many students at Monday’s rally, this will be their first chance to vote in a gubernatorial election. Students supporting Spanberger said her policies represent the future they want to see.

“I’m really passionate about reproductive rights and reproductive freedom and the cost of living and I think that she has a really good vision for Virginia,” said Lauren Prattis, a VCU student.

“Top issues are education and freedom of education,” Eric Hansen, also a VCU student, said.

Students told 12 On Your Side there’s an increased energy amongst them and their peers for this election. It’s a sentiment Spanberger hopes will turn into votes on Tuesday.

“We need you to get all of your friends, your family members, anyone else out who may cast a ballot, we need them to do it,” Spanberger said.

Both Spanberger and Earle-Sears held a handful of rallies across Virginia on Monday, as they push through the final leg of campaigning before Election Day.
