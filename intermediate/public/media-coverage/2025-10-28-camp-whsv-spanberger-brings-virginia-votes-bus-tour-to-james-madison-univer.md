By Ryan Hartnett

HARRISONBURG, Va. (WHSV) – Abigail Spanberger, the Democratic candidate for Virginia governor, made a pit stop in Harrisonburg Tuesday afternoon on the campus of James Madison University.

Spanberger is in the home stretch of her gubernatorial campaign, and with Election Day just one week away, she spoke with students and full-time community members about the issues impacting Virginia most and how she intends to address them if elected.

“I have rolled out plans to lower costs in housing, healthcare, and energy because I know that this is how we create greater opportunity and address the concerns that are top of mind for so many Virginians,” Spanberger said.

Joining the gubernatorial candidate at the rally was House of Delegates candidate Andrew Payton, who said he believes that student voters at JMU will elevate him to victory over Tony Wilt, the Republican incumbent who’s held the seat since 2010.

“I will work to ensure that you have the opportunities to succeed, I will work to raise wages, lower costs, and to make life more affordable for working Virginians,” said Payton, the Democrat running for the 34th district House of Delegates seat.

But with most of those in attendance having already voted early, Spanberger encouraged them to help get those who have yet to do so to the ballot box on November 4.

“A week from today we get to go to the polls, or in this case bring our friends to the polls, and a week from today we will win,” Spanberger said.
