**DOWNLOADABLE PHOTOS: Spanberger Joins Fish Bowl Classic Parade in Portsmouth**

NORFOLK, Va. — On Saturday, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger joined the Fish Bowl Classic Parade in Portsmouth and participated in the pregame coin toss at the Old Dominion University vs. Liberty University football game at S.B. Ballard Stadium in Norfolk.

“Saturday was an incredible day of meeting Virginians across Hampton Roads and spending time together in community. I’m grateful to every Virginian who took the time to share with me their vision for Virginia’s future and their biggest concerns,” said Spanberger. “I spoke with Virginians who are worried about how they’ll pay their rent, how they’ll make their mortgage payments, and the dollar amount that will show up on their next energy bill. In the coming weeks, I will continue to speak with Virginians across the Commonwealth about the real challenges facing their families and communities — and I look forward to sharing my plans to make Virginia more affordable, strengthen Virginia’s economy, and put Virginia’s public schools on a path toward being the best in America.”

Spanberger kicked off the day riding on a float and meeting Virginians at the 78th Annual Fish Bowl Classic Parade in Portsmouth. Spanberger participated in the festivities alongside Virginia Senate President Pro Tempore L. Louise Lucas and Virginia House Speaker Don Scott (both who represent Portsmouth), Democratic Lieutenant Governor nominee Senator Ghazala Hashmi, and Chairman of the Democratic Party of Virginia Senator Lamont Bagby.

Spanberger spent Saturday evening participating in the ceremonial coin toss ahead of Old Dominion University hosting Liberty University for an in-state rivalry football matchup. Spanberger spent time with Old Dominion students and university faculty, administrators, and staff.
