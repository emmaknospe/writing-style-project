Spanberger: “We Need a Governor Who Can Try to Steady Things and Create as Much Certainty as Possible”

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger sat down to discuss how as Virginia’s next Governor she will contend with the major challenges facing the Commonwealth — the high cost of living, chaotic attacks on Virginia’s workforce and economy coming from Washington, and the devastating impacts of Medicaid cuts — with NBC12 in Richmond.

Click here to watch Spanberger’s full interview.

During the interview, Spanberger made clear her administration will put Virginians first and work to lower the rising costs they face with her Affordable Virginia Plan, especially at the pharmacy counter and doctor’s office, in the housing market, and on energy bills.

Spanberger emphasized how her administration will work to lower energy costs by increasing local generation.

“We also need to contend with long term challenges of energy production — energy costs are an issue I hear about across Virginia,” said Spanberger.

Spanberger also addressed attacks on Virginia’s workforce and economy from Washington head on and made clear that she will always stand up for Virginians — no matter who is in the White House.

“The next Governor is going to have to contend with the immediacy of challenges that we have seen because of the chaos coming out of Washington,” continued Spanberger.

Amid the chaos, Spanberger will be a leader who creates stability and opportunity for Virginians.

“We need a Governor who can try to steady things and create as much certainty as possible,” said Spanberger.

And while the Republican tax bill threatens healthcare access and hospitals across the Commonwealth, Spanberger underscored the impact these cuts will have as healthcare access is threatened for more than 322,000 Virginians and hospitals across the Commonwealth will lose approximately $26 billion, putting at least six rural hospitals at risk of closure.

“That bill will impact the healthcare of hundreds of thousands of Virginians. It will lead to the closure of rural hospitals,” furthered Spanberger.

BACKGROUND

This year, Spanberger has already announced her Affordable Virginia Plan, Growing Virginia Plan, and Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan day one to address challenges in Virginia’s cost of living, economy, and schools.

As a member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was named the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.
