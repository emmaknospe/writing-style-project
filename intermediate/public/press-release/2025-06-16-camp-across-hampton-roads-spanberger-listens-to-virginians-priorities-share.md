On Saturday, Spanberger Listened to Elected Officials & Community Members in Franklin, Suffolk, Norfolk, Hampton, and Newport News about the Challenges They’re Facing and Shared Her Vision for Virginia’s Future

RICHMOND, Va. — On Saturday, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger met with families, Veterans, and community members across Hampton Roads.

Spanberger listened to Virginians’ ideas, thoughts, and concerns about the future of the Commonwealth — and outlined her “Affordable Virginia Plan” to lower healthcare and prescription drug, housing, and energy costs. Spanberger spent time in Franklin, Suffolk, Norfolk, Hampton, and Newport News.

“Saturday was an energizing day spent in Franklin, Suffolk, Norfolk, Newport News, and Hampton — celebrating both Flag Day and the upcoming Juneteenth holiday while engaging directly with Virginians,” said Spanberger. “At every stop, we exchanged ideas about how to make Virginia more affordable and strengthen our communities. These conversations are driving this campaign, and as Governor, I’ll remain focused on delivering solutions that reflect the needs of Virginians across Hampton Roads and the Commonwealth.”

CELEBRATING FLAG DAY

In Franklin, Spanberger kicked the day off by celebrating Flag Day with local Democratic committee members from Franklin, Southampton, Isle of Wight, and Suffolk. During the event, Spanberger shared her plan to make Virginia more affordable and made clear the stakes of this year’s statewide and House of Delegates elections.

DEL. NADARIUS CLARK’S ANNUAL FISH FRY IN SUFFOLK

Later on Saturday morning, Spanberger met Virginians at Delegate Nadarius Clark’s (HD-84) Annual Fish Fry in Suffolk. Spanberger spoke with community members about the issues that matter most to them and their communities — making Virginia more affordable, strengthening Virginia’s schools, and making sure that everyone has an opportunity to thrive in the Commonwealth.

JUNETEENTH CELEBRATION IN NORFOLK

In Norfolk, Spanberger gathered with Virginians and local elected leaders to honor Juneteenth. At Juneteenth Norfolk, Spanberger reflected on the history of the holiday and underscored the themes of resilience and freedom it celebrates.

GREATER HAMPTON ROADS BLACK DEMOCRATS EVENT

Later in the afternoon, Spanberger met with members of the Greater Hampton Roads Black Democrats in Hampton. Throughout her remarks and conversations, Spanberger discussed the need to lower costs across the Commonwealth — especially at the doctor’s office and pharmacy counter, in the housing market, and on Virginians’ energy bills — as well as the need to strengthen Virginia’s schools, and defend Virginians’ freedoms.

NEWPORT NEWS ORGANIZING DINNER

Spanberger ended the day by meeting with organizers and volunteers in Newport News, expressing her gratitude for all their hard work knocking doors and spreading the word about her campaign across Hampton Roads.

BACKGROUND

Spanberger served three terms in the U.S. House of Representatives from January 2019 to January 2025. During her three terms in Congress, Spanberger was consistently ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — tracking narcotics traffickers and working money laundering cases. She then served as a CIA case officer — working undercover on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters in Virginia public schools.

Abigail Spanberger announced her campaign to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia in November 2023.
