RICHMOND, VA — Governor Abigail Spanberger today highlighted her administration’s continued focus on lowering costs for Virginia families, growing the Commonwealth’s economy, and strengthening Virginia’s public schools.

“I came into office with a focus on delivering for families by building a stronger, more affordable Virginia. We are making rapid progress toward that goal — announcing hundreds of millions of dollars in new business investment in communities across Virginia, launching our cross-Commonwealth school tour to make sure every student can get a public education that helps them reach their full potential, and soon signing legislation into law to lower costs for Virginia families.

“We are just getting started. Across the Commonwealth, I continue to hear from Virginians who are feeling the squeeze of high prices, are worried about the uncertainty coming out of Washington, and want to make sure their kids are being set up for success. But I know that our Commonwealth can meet any challenge. I look forward to continuing to partner with legislators and local communities to deliver results for Virginia families, Virginia schools, and Virginia businesses.”

In the opening months of her administration, Governor Spanberger has remained laser-focused on making life less expensive for Virginians.

In December, Governor Spanberger announced the “Affordable Virginia Agenda” — her key legislative priorities with General Assembly leaders to lower housing, healthcare, and energy costs for Virginians. Every bill in the agenda has passed the House of Delegates, Senate, or both.

The Governor also signed day one executive orders directing her administration to identify ways to reduce living expenses for families, reduce healthcare costs for Virginians, and encourage the development of more housing to lower housing costs.

As Governor Spanberger focuses on lowering costs for families, Virginians are feeling more optimistic about the future of the Commonwealth’s economy.

According to data released recently by Roanoke College, all three components of the Virginia Index of Consumer Sentiment have increased in recent months — with the Commonwealth seeing the largest single-period rise in consumer expectations in more than a year. In the analysis of the improving data, Roanoke College specifically highlighted the Governor’s policy priorities on affordability.

Governor Spanberger is working alongside legislators and local communities to attract new investment in the Commonwealth.

During her first 50 days in office, the Governor has announced $575 million in new business investment in communities across the Commonwealth — including the second-largest economic development deal in Southern Virginia history. In total, these investments will create nearly 2,000 new jobs for Virginians.

Finally, Governor Spanberger is hearing directly from parents and educators about how to strengthen Virginia’s public schools.

Last week, the Governor launched her Administration’s “Commonwealth Listening Tour” in Hampton with a roundtable discussion focused on setting Virginia students up for success and supporting Virginia teachers who help kids reach their full potential.

On her first day in office, Governor Spanberger signed an executive order focused on making Virginia’s public schools the best in the nation. As part of that executive order, the Governor directed the Secretary of Education and Superintendent of Public Instruction to host this Commonwealth-wide listening tour to hear directly from Virginia teachers, parents, school leaders, and students.
