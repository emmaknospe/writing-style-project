Earlier this Week, Spanberger Was Endorsed by Former Virginia Governor Ralph Northam and U.S. Reps. Beyer, Connolly, & Wexton

HENRICO, Va. — State and local officials from across Virginia today endorsed U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger to serve as Virginia’s next Governor, following the launch of her campaign earlier this week.

Today’s endorsement slate from Members of the Virginia General Assembly and local officials reflects the broad and growing support for Spanberger’s campaign — from Hampton Roads to Northern Virginia to Virginia’s Seventh District to Southwest Virginia.

The following Virginia elected officials have now announced their support for Spanberger to serve as Virginia’s 75th Governor:

Spanberger thanked these public servants for their commitment to the future of Virginia and for their strong support of her campaign.

“Our success as a Commonwealth requires leaders at all levels of government — federal, state, and local — to work together. In the four days since launching our campaign, I’ve been humbled and honored by the tremendous wave of support from Virginia leaders,” said Spanberger. “Virginia is home to incredible public servants who truly want to get things done for our Commonwealth’s families, communities, and overall economy. As Governor, I look forward to working tirelessly with these officials to make it happen.”

BACKGROUND

Spanberger launched her gubernatorial campaign on Monday. On Tuesday, U.S. Rep. Jennifer Wexton endorsed Spanberger — and on Wednesday, former Virginia Governor Ralph Northam, U.S. Rep. Don Beyer (VA-08), and U.S. Rep. Gerry Connolly (VA-11) endorsed Spanberger.

U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger (VA-07) is serving her third term in the U.S. House of Representatives. In Congress, she serves on the U.S. House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence and the U.S. House Agriculture Committee.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — tracking narcotics traffickers and working money laundering cases — and as a CIA case officer — working undercover on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are raising three school-aged daughters in Virginia.
