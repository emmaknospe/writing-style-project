VIRGINIA BEACH, Va. — On Saturday, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger wrapped up her “Span Virginia Bus Tour” with stops including Williamsburg, Newport News, Norfolk, and Virginia Beach.

Throughout the day, Spanberger met with local elected leaders, visited small businesses, launched “Veterans for Spanberger,” and listened to Virginians discuss the issues that matter most to them throughout the stops. Spanberger emphasized her commitment to always putting Virginia and Virginians first — including by making sure Virginia’s more than 600,000 Veterans receive the benefits they deserve, protecting Virginians’ fundamental freedoms, and lowering healthcare, housing, and energy costs through her “Affordable Virginia Plan.”

WILLIAMSBURG

Spanberger — along with state Senator Ghazala Hashmi and former Delegate Jay Jones, the Democratic nominees for Lieutenant Governor and Attorney General — began the final day of the “Span Virginia Bus Tour” by joining the Historic Triangle Democrats’ breakfast and canvass kickoff. During the event, Spanberger shared her vision for Virginia’s future, including lowering Virginians’ costs, strengthening Virginia’s economy, putting Virginia’s public schools on a path to being the best in the country, and protecting Virginians’s fundamental freedoms.

NEWPORT NEWS

Newport News Mayor Philip Jones joined Spanberger — along with Hashmi and Jones — to visit local businesses and discuss how the chaos coming out of Washington is impacting Main Street Virginia. Spanberger then joined Mayor Jones as he read the city’s annual Pride proclamation and made clear that as Governor, she will always stand up for Virginians’ fundamental freedoms — including for LGBTQ+ Virginians.

NORFOLK

Spanberger, Hashmi, and Jones, then headed to Norfolk to launch “Veterans for Spanberger” alongside former Governor Ralph Northam, Speaker Don Scott, local elected leaders, and members of “Veterans for Spanberger.” Spanberger made clear that her administration will make sure that Virginia’s Veterans receive the benefits they’ve earned and that the Commonwealth is the best place for Veterans, active-duty servicemembers, and military families to call home.

VIRGINIA BEACH

Spanberger completed the “Span Virginia Bus Tour” with multiple stops in Virginia Beach, speaking at the Filipino-American Friendship Day festival and rallying with hundreds of Virginians on the Virginia Beach Oceanfront. Across the stops, Spanberger continued to emphasize that, as Governor, she will always put Virginia and Virginians first.

BACKGROUND

Spanberger announced the launch of her “Span Virginia Bus Tour” at a rally at her high school alma mater, J.R. Tucker High School.

Along the “Span Virginia Bus Tour,” Spanberger is touting her plan to lower costs for Virginians — the “Affordable Virginia Plan.” Over the last month, she has held events across Virginia outlining this vision to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

Spanberger served three terms in the U.S. House of Representatives from January 2019 to January 2025. During her three terms in Congress, Spanberger was consistently ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — working narcotics and money laundering cases. She then served as a CIA case officer — working on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters in Virginia public schools.
