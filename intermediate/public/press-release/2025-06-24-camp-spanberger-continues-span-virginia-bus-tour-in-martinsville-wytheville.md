ROANOKE, Va. — On Monday, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger continued her “Span Virginia Bus Tour” across the Commonwealth, making stops in Martinsville, Wytheville, Blacksburg, and Roanoke.

Spanberger spent the day listening to Virginians discuss the issues that matter most to them and sharing her vision for the future of Virginia — including her “Affordable Virginia Plan” to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs. Additionally, she outlined her commitment to protecting Virginia jobs, putting Virginia on the path toward having the best public schools in America, and defending Virginians’ fundamental freedoms.

MARTINSVILLE

Spanberger started the third day of her “Span Virginia Bus Tour” by grabbing breakfast with Virginians at Be Wiched Diner in Martinsville. Going from table to table at the diner, Spanberger outlined her plans to make Virginia more affordable by lowering costs at the pharmacy counter, in the housing market, and on their energy bills.

WYTHEVILLE

Day Three of the “Span Virginia Bus Tour” continued as Spanberger spoke with Virginians at Seven Sisters Brewing Company in Wytheville, focusing on how her administration will deliver results and strengthen Southwest Virginia’s access to affordable healthcare, reliable infrastructure, and strong public schools and job opportunities.

BLACKSBURG

At the opening of a joint field office for the Spanberger for Governor campaign and Lily Franklin, candidate for Virginia House of Delegates District 41, Spanberger and Democratic nominee for Lieutenant Governor Ghazala Hashmi highlighted the stakes of the 2025 Virginia elections. In her remarks, she highlighted her commitment to lowering costs for Blacksburg residents and reiterated Virginia Democrats’ commitment to competing in all 100 House districts on this year’s ballot, from far Southwest Virginia to the Eastern Shore.

ROANOKE

The “Span Virginia Bus Tour” then rolled into Roanoke, where Spanberger rallied with supporters at the Virginia Museum of Transportation. At the event, Hashmi introduced Spanberger, outlining their shared commitment to putting Virginians first, making Virginia more affordable, and strengthening Virginia’s public schools.

BACKGROUND

Spanberger announced the launch of her “Span Virginia Bus Tour” last week at a rally at her high school alma mater, J.R. Tucker High School.

Along the “Span Virginia Bus Tour,” Spanberger is touting her plan to lower costs for Virginians — the “Affordable Virginia Plan.” Over the last month, she has held events across Virginia outlining this vision to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

Spanberger served three terms in the U.S. House of Representatives from January 2019 to January 2025. During her three terms in Congress, Spanberger was consistently ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — working narcotics and money laundering cases. She then served as a CIA case officer — working on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters in Virginia public schools.
