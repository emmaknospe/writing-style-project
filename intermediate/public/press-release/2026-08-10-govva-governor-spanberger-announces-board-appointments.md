RICHMOND, VA — Governor Abigail Spanberger today announced the following board appointments.

“I am proud to announce this outstanding group of Virginians who have stepped up to use their expertise in business, healthcare, economic development, and conservation to serve the Commonwealth,” said Governor Abigail Spanberger. “I have full confidence that these appointees will work to lower costs, invest in our healthcare systems, and protect our natural resources.”
