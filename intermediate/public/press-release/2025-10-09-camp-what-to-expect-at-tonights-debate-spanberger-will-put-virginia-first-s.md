While Spanberger Is Focused on Lowering Costs for Virginia Families, Sears Praises President Trump’s Attacks on Virginia Jobs & Backs His Price-Hiking Agenda

NORFOLK, Va. — During this evening’s televised, statewide gubernatorial debate hosted by WAVY-TV and Norfolk State University, Abigail Spanberger will drive a clear message focused on the issues that matter most to Virginia voters — making Virginia more affordable and growing Virginia’s economy.

Ahead of the debate, Spanberger for Governor Campaign Manager Samson Signori released the following statement.

“Tonight’s debate will make clear the contrast in this race — Abigail Spanberger is focused on solving the real challenges Virginia families face, while Winsome Earle-Sears has already shown that she will always put President Trump first.

“Virginians across the Commonwealth are echoing what Abigail has been saying all year — that Virginians want a Governor who is focused on making Virginia more affordable. That’s exactly why Abigail has laid out her plans to bring down housing, healthcare, and energy costs — and why she’ll get to work on Day One to get it done.

“Tonight, Virginians will also see that Winsome Earle-Sears doesn’t have the backbone to stand up to President Trump’s attacks on Virginia. Instead, Sears is going to lie about Abigail’s record and mislead Virginians about her own — but she won’t be able to cover up her steadfast support for President Trump as he drives up costs for families, threatens Virginians’ healthcare, and kills Virginia jobs.

“Unlike her opponent, Abigail will never put party or politics ahead of the people of Virginia. I’m glad that Virginians will have this opportunity to hear exactly how Abigail will put them first.”

BACKGROUND

WHAT TO EXPECT FROM ABIGAIL SPANBERGER: During tonight’s debate, Abigail Spanberger will drive how she will get to work to deliver for the Commonwealth and always put Virginia first.

WHAT TO EXPECT FROM WINSOME EARLE-SEARS: On the debate stage, Virginians will hear Winsome Earle-Sears’ extreme positions firsthand — and will not hear any real plans to address Virginians’ top-of-mind concerns.
