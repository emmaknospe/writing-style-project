RICHMOND, VA — Governor Abigail Spanberger today announced the following board appointments.

“Today, I am appointing Virginians who share my commitment to serving the people of the Commonwealth,” said Governor Abigail Spanberger. “Together, these leaders will strengthen our communities, enhance the essential public services Virginians count on, and support our economy as they serve across our agriculture and forestry, public safety and homeland security, and finance boards. I know they will bring experience, vision, and common dedication in building the safer, stronger, and more affordable future that all Virginia families deserve.”
