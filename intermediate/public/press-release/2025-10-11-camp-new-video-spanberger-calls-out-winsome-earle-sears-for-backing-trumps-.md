Spanberger: “Virginia Deserves a Governor Who Puts Virginians First”

RICHMOND, Va. — In a new video, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today called out Winsome Earle-Sears for continuing to back President Trump as his administration yesterday laid off more than 4,000 federal workers amid the government shutdown.

At Thursday’s debate, Winsome Earle-Sears outright refused to call on President Trump to stop this chaos when asked directly by the moderators. That refusal comes after Earle-Sears said DOGE cuts and attacks on Virginia’s economy were not a “real issue” in a CNN interview earlier this year.

Click here to watch Spanberger’s video response, and read the transcript below:

—

SPANBERGER: “Yesterday, the Trump Administration announced they will begin mass firings of Virginia’s federal workers.

“Let’s be clear about this is: it’s DOGE 2.0. This comes as I continue to hear from Virginia Veterans, federal workers, and families who are hurting because of President Trump’s attacks on our Commonwealth.

“Just this Thursday, at our debate in Norfolk, I called out my opponent for refusing to tell Trump to stop this chaos.

“I believe Virginia deserves a Governor who puts Virginians first.

“While my opponent stands loyal to Trump and isn’t calling out this chaos, I will continue to call out the President — and to call on the President — to stop attacking Virginia’s workforce, to focus on bringing people together, and to put an end to this government shutdown”

—

Yesterday, Spanberger reacted to the Trump Administration “recklessly escalating” their longstanding attacks on Virginia federal workers by implementing mass layoffs amid the government shutdown — and made clear that unlike her opponent, she will always put Virginians first as their next Governor.
