During the Tour, Spanberger Heard From Area Veterans & Learned More About Tech For Troops’ Workforce Training Programs

HENRICO, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today toured Tech for Troops — a Virginia-based, Veteran-staffed, and Veteran-operated non-profit organization that works to provide Veterans and their families with digital skills training.

During her visit, Spanberger joined Tech for Troops leadership and Virginia Veterans to discuss how training programs like these can help Veterans get ahead. As the next Governor of Virginia, Spanberger will work to expand opportunities and make sure Virginia Veterans receive the support they deserve. Spanberger also joined an in-house training session to observe first-hand how the organization’s programs help Veterans prepare for future career opportunities.

During her visit, Spanberger also discussed her vision to make sure Virginia is the best place in America for Veterans, active-duty servicemembers, and military families to call home.

“Virginia is proud of its deep connections to the military, and we show our gratitude by having their backs. As the next Governor of Virginia, I will put our Veterans first — and that includes offering support in the job search and helping our heroes take care of their families,” said Spanberger. “All Virginia Veterans should enjoy a job with dignity, respect, and strong benefits. My administration will expand pathways for Veterans to enter the workforce, reskill, and upskill.”

In the U.S. House, Spanberger led bipartisan legislation that was signed into law to provide refurbished surplus federal computers to Veterans as they look for new employment opportunities in the civilian workforce.

“We are grateful Abigail Spanberger took the time to visit our Henrico facility today, and we look forward to continuing to address issues facing our Veterans together,” said Tech for Troops President & CEO Mark Casper.

BACKGROUND

As the next Governor of Virginia, Spanberger will work to expand opportunities for Virginia’s bravest citizens and make sure Virginia Veterans receive the best possible care — and stand up to attempts to slash support for our nation’s heroes.

Last month, Spanberger launched “Veterans for Spanberger” in Norfolk alongside Veterans from across the Commonwealth. During the event, Virginia Veterans praised Spanberger’s commitment to both stand up for Virginia’s Veterans as their benefits are threatened and make sure the Commonwealth is the best place for Veterans, active-duty servicemembers, and military families to call home.

As a Member of Congress, Abigail brought Republicans and Democrats together to expand healthcare coverage and improve job opportunities for Virginia’s Veterans.

Abigail voted to pass the bipartisan Honoring Our PACT Act, which expanded U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) healthcare and disability benefits for thousands of Virginians who were exposed to burn pits and other toxic substances during the Vietnam War, Gulf War, and post-9/11 eras. The bipartisan law included legislation — named after a Veteran from Louisa County — Abigail led to investigate the health impacts of jet fuel exposure on servicemembers.

Spanberger also led a bipartisan push — that was ultimately signed into law by the President — to recognize America’s Atomic Veterans every year on July 16, now recognized as National Atomic Veterans Day. With many of these Veterans’ service and sacrifice going unrecognized for decades due to the secrecy of their mission, Spanberger made sure they are honored for the critical role they played in protecting our nation.
