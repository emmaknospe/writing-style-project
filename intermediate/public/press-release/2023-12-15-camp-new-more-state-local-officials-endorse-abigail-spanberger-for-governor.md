HENRICO, Va. — Today, more Virginia state and local leaders are endorsing U.S. Representative Abigail Spanberger to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia.

Today’s endorsements reflect Spanberger’s demonstrated commitment to tackling a wide range of issues as Governor, as well as her strong support from all corners of the Commonwealth.

The following Virginia elected officials today endorsed Abigail Spanberger to serve as Virginia’s next Governor.

Senator-Elect Stella Pekarsky, Virginia Senate District 36

Del. Karrie Delaney, Virginia House of Delegates, District 9

Martin A. Thomas, Jr., Vice-Mayor, City of Norfolk

Monaé Nickerson, Vice-Mayor, Town of Dumfries

Tiffany Boyle, Commissioner of the Revenue, City of Newport News

Todd D. Divers, Commissioner of the Revenue, City of Charlottesville

Dr. Pamela Yeung, Chairwoman, Stafford County Board of Supervisors

Matt de Ferranti, Member, Arlington County Board

Jay Fisette, Former Chair, Arlington County Board

Dr. Deborah Frazier, Spotsylvania County Board of Supervisors

Selonia Miles, Chair Pro Tem & Councilwoman, Town of Dumfries
