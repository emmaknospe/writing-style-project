HENRICO, Va. — The first and largest progressive group of Veterans — VoteVets PAC — today endorsed U.S. Representative Abigail Spanberger to serve as the next Governor of Virginia.

VoteVets PAC praised Spanberger for her consistent work on behalf of Virginia’s Veterans — including her support for the bipartisan Honoring Our PACT Act. This law marks the largest expansion of U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) benefits in a generation, and it includes Spanberger’s measure to investigate the negative impacts of jet-fuel exposure on the long-term health of America’s Veterans.

“Abigail Spanberger will be an incredible Governor, and we’re proud to endorse her,” said U.S. Marine Corps Veteran Travis Tazelaar, Political Director, VoteVets PAC. “Her career has been about her commitment to protecting our Constitution and delivering for the people she serves. Her ability to rise above division and get things done for her community, Commonwealth, and country is the spirit of service that we need more now than ever. She’s been a great friend to the Veterans community and to all those who serve in uniform. She’s had our backs, always, and we’re going to have hers.”

“Virginia has a proud record of military service, and our Commonwealth is home to tremendous individuals and organizations dedicated to supporting those who have borne the battle,” said Spanberger. “VoteVets PAC understands the mission ahead — delivering on our promises to our Veterans while protecting the Constitution they swore to protect. I thank them for their support and their unwavering commitment to our bravest citizens.”

Since arriving in Congress, Spanberger has also led bipartisan efforts that have been signed into law to help Veterans access new job opportunities as they transition to the civilian workforce, as well as to recognize the service of America’s Atomic Veterans. Additionally, she has hosted many Veteran-focused events to hear concerns directly from Virginia’s Veterans, active-duty servicemembers, and military families.

“In Congress, I’ve worked tirelessly to make sure our Veterans receive the benefits, recognition, and healthcare they’ve earned through their service,” continued Spanberger. “And as Governor, I will work with both parties to increase career opportunities for our Veterans, strengthen access to mental health resources, hold the VA accountable when necessary, and make our Commonwealth an even better place to live for our military families.”

Spanberger is the daughter, daughter in-law, and granddaughter of U.S. Armed Forces Veterans.

BACKGROUND

Earlier this month, Spanberger announced her campaign to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia. Since launching her campaign, Spanberger has been endorsed by former Virginia Governor Ralph Northam; U.S. Representatives Jennifer Wexton (VA-10), Gerry Connolly (VA-11), and Don Beyer (VA-08); former U.S. Representatives Elaine Luria (VA-02) and Tom Perriello (VA-05); and dozens of Virginia state and local officials.

Spanberger is serving her third term in the U.S. House of Representatives. In Congress, she serves on the U.S. House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence and the U.S. House Agriculture Committee.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — tracking narcotics traffickers and working money laundering cases — and as a CIA case officer — working undercover on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters.
