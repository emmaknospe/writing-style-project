In Front of a Crowd of More Than 500 Virginians, Spanberger Celebrated Her Virginia Values & Outlined Her Vision for the Commonwealth’s Future

**CLICK HERE for Downloadable Photos**

HENRICO, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger last night rallied with supporters at her high school alma mater, J.R. Tucker High School in Henrico County, Virginia.

Standing in front of a crowd of more than 500 Virginians, Spanberger celebrated her deep Virginia values that fostered her commitment to serving her community and her country. Spanberger outlined her vision for the future of the Commonwealth — a clear vision that will allow more families to put down roots in Virginia by lowering costs, strengthening schools, and putting the needs of Virginians above chaos and partisan politics.

“Being back here tonight at my old high school reminds me why this fight is worth fighting. I was lucky to have amazing opportunities here. I was lucky to have educators who cared deeply about the future of their students. I was lucky. And now, I want to make sure that every student, every family, and every community in Virginia has those same opportunities — not because of luck, but because of good governance,” said Spanberger during the event. “Those are the stakes of this election moving forward.”

Following the Pledge of Allegiance — recited by students from Springfield Park Elementary School, Senator Schuyler VanValkenburg (SD-16) and Delegate Destiny LeVere Bolling (HD-80) kicked off the event by highlighting Spanberger’s record of delivering results and her connection to the community that raised her in Henrico.

“As our next Governor, Abigail is someone who will always put Virginia first — because Virginia is her home,” said VanValkenburg. “It’s where she was raised, it’s where she went to school — right here at Tucker High School, and it’s where she brought back her family to raise her kids and put down even deeper roots. She is a Henrico hometown hero.”

“Throughout her entire career of public service, she has a record of putting Virginia’s families first,” said LeVere Bolling. “In Congress, she brought people together to get things done, and she continuously delivered results. She delivered for our Veterans, law enforcement officers, and more importantly, for all our neighbors. And now, she’s focused more than ever on building on that work by addressing the issues that face Virginians in every part of the Commonwealth.”

Spanberger was also joined by Marques Jones, a local Multiple Sclerosis advocate and longtime Henrico County resident, who highlighted Spanberger’s commitment to delivering for all Virginians.

“In Congress, Abigail always voted with every Virginian in mind — including people who live with disabilities, both visible and invisible, like me. As someone living with Multiple Sclerosis, I knew I could always count on my friend Abigail to support commonsense bipartisan legislation that helped increase medical research funding, increase access to healthcare, reform drug pricing, and more,” said Jones. “And when elected Governor in November, Abigail will continue to fight for us.”

Spanberger was introduced by her former Spanish teacher at J.R. Tucker High School, Millie Green, who spoke about Spanberger’s upbringing in Henrico County Public Schools.

“Abigail Spanberger represents the very best of what education can inspire and what service can achieve. I am deeply proud to have played a small part in her journey, and I’m even prouder to stand here tonight with my former student — a J.R. Tucker graduate — and call her my friend,” said Green. “I will be even prouder when she makes history yet again.”

During Monday night’s event, Spanberger also announced that she will travel across all 11 of Virginia’s congressional districts on her eight-day, 40-plus-stop “Span Virginia Bus Tour.” Along the tour, Spanberger will tour small businesses and Main Streets, visit Virginia historic and cultural sites, and rally with local leaders and community members as she shares her vision for the future of the Commonwealth.

For more information about the “Span Virginia Bus Tour,” please visit abigailspanberger.com/bus.

BACKGROUND

Over the last month, Spanberger has traveled across Virginia to announce her plan — the “Affordable Virginia Plan” — to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

Spanberger secured the Democratic nomination for Governor of Virginia on April, 3, 2025.

Spanberger served three terms in the U.S. House of Representatives from January 2019 to January 2025. During her three terms in Congress, Spanberger was consistently ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — working narcotics and money laundering cases. She then served as a CIA case officer — working on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases.

She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters in Virginia public schools.
