RICHMOND, Va. — Today, January 26, Governor Spanberger will meet with Virginia Department of Transportation (VDOT) crews, Virginia State Police (VSP) troopers, Virginia Department of Emergency Management (VDEM) representatives, and other first responders at the VDOT Richmond District Office.

Governor Spanberger will receive a briefing at VDOT’s Transportation Operations Center about the state’s coordinated response to this winter storm. The Governor will also thank these public servants — including VDOT mechanics and safety teams who are getting equipment back on the road — for their work to keep Virginians safe during the winter weather.

WHO: Governor Abigail Spanberger

WHEN: Monday, January 26, 2026 at 2:30 P.M.

WHERE: Virginia Department of Transportation Richmond District Office 
2430 Pine Forest Drive 
South Chesterfield, Virginia 23834

*To access the VDOT Operations Center, take the second left at the VDOT sign before the train tracks and pull around the far side of the glass office building to the back of the complex where the main entrance is located.

PRESS: RSVP is required for the event. Please email press@governor.virginia.gov to RSVP by 2:00 P.M. on Monday, January 26, 2026 and include the names and news agency of those attending.
