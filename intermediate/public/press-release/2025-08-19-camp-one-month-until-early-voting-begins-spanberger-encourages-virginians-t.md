Early Voting in Virginia’s General Elections Runs from Tuesday, September 19 Through Saturday, November 1

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today — one month until polls open for early voting in Virginia — encouraged Virginians to check their voter registration status ahead of this year’s general election and make a plan to vote.

“In just one month, Virginians will begin heading to the polls to cast their votes in this year’s general elections. As Virginians continue to face unprecedented challenges — high costs across the board, attacks on our workforce and economy, and last-in-the-nation progress to combat learning loss, Virginians have an opportunity to make a choice. Virginians can choose leaders who will put Virginians — not partisan loyalty — first. From now through Election Day on November 4, I will continue to work tirelessly to listen to Virginians and make the case for earning their vote.

“I encourage every eligible Virginian to register to vote or make sure their registration is up to date, double check their polling place, and make a plan to vote. As we prepare for the start of early voting on September 19, I am grateful for the countless election officials and volunteers who do the hard work of making sure our elections remain free, safe, and secure — and I thank them in advance for their efforts this election season.”

EARLY VOTING RESOURCES

Virginians can vote early, either in person or by mail, in the 2025 general elections from Friday, September 19 through Saturday, November 1. Election Day is Tuesday, November 4, 2025.

If Virginians have questions about how to register to vote or want to check their registration status, they can click here.

Virginians can visit the Virginia Department of Elections website to find their early voting location and operating hours.
