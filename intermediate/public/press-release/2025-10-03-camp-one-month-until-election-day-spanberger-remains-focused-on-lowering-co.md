Spanberger: “As Virginia’s Next Governor, I Will Get to Work on Day One to Bring Down Costs for Virginia Families”

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today — one month ahead of Election Day — encouraged Virginians to make their voices heard and make a plan to vote early or on Election Day.

“Virginians want a Governor who puts them first. As I travel across the Commonwealth, I continue to hear from Virginians who are working harder than ever, but are still worried about making ends meet — in the face of escalating attacks out of Washington on Virginia’s federal workforce, Virginians’ access to affordable healthcare, and Virginia’s overall economy. As Virginia’s next Governor, I will get to work on day one to bring down costs for Virginia families, strengthen Virginia’s economy, and make sure Virginia’s public schools are the best in America.

“This November — and while polls are open early, Virginians have an opportunity to make their voices heard. From now through Election Day, I will continue to listen to families across every corner of our Commonwealth and make the case for earning their vote.”

BACKGROUND

Earlier this year, Spanberger rolled out her plan to lower costs for Virginians across the board — her Affordable Virginia Plan, which focuses on lowering healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

Spanberger has a plan to lower healthcare costs. Spanberger’s plan is focused on saving Virginians money by cracking down on the pharmacy benefits managers (PBMs) responsible for inflating the cost of Virginians’ prescription drugs, allowing more Virginians to use the pharmacy of their choice, improving hospital price transparency, and stopping predatory billing practices that harm Virginians.

Spanberger has a plan to lower housing costs. As Governor, Spanberger will work to lower costs by giving cities and counties the flexibility they need to build more housing, cutting red tape and reducing burdensome regulations that slow down housing construction, and directing investments towards housing Virginia families can afford — including by providing incentives for new construction that increases the supply of housing attainable to first-time homeowners and middle-class families.

Spanberger has a plan to lower Virginians’ energy costs. Spanberger’s plan is focused on making Virginia more energy independent by increasing local generation, making sure data centers don’t drive up energy costs for everyone else in Virginia, and addressing regulatory issues to speed up new energy projects, drive down costs, and increase certainty.

The Spanberger campaign recently released a TV and digital ad, titled “Raise Costs,” highlighting Winsome Earle-Sears’ praise for the Republican bill signed into law in July by President Trump. Sears said the legislation — which is projected to raise Virginians’ healthcare costs, mortgage payments, and utility bills — “does so many great things.”

Spanberger has also announced her Growing Virginia Plan and Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan — showing that her administration will have a plan on day one to address challenges facing Virginia’s economy and schools.

As a Member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to deliver results for Virginians — and was named the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.

VOTER RESOURCES

The deadline to register to vote in Virginia is Friday, October 24. Election Day is Tuesday, November 4, 2025.

For this year’s election, there are three easy ways to cast your vote — by mail, early in person, or on Election Day.

If Virginians have questions about how to register to vote or want to check their registration status, they can visit the Virginia Department of Elections website.
