HENRICO, Va. — Today, more Virginia state and local leaders from across the Commonwealth endorsed U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia.

Today’s endorsements reflect Spanberger’s demonstrated commitment to tackling a wide range of issues — including strengthening public education, protecting reproductive freedom, preventing gun violence, supporting Virginia’s small businesses, and much more.

The following Virginia elected officials today announced their support for Spanberger to serve as Virginia’s next Governor.

Virginia House of Delegates

Del. Richard C. “Rip” Sullivan Jr., Virginia House of Delegates, District 6

Del. Paul Krizek, Virginia House of Delegates, District 16

Virginia House of Delegates

She is the clear choice to protect reproductive freedom, prevent gun violence in our communities, and make landmark progress in tackling climate change while creating a more sustainable Virginia for our children and grandchildren.”

BACKGROUND

U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger (VA-07) is serving her third term in the U.S. House of Representatives. In Congress, she serves on the U.S. House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence and the U.S. House Agriculture Committee.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — tracking narcotics traffickers and working money laundering cases — and as a CIA case officer — working undercover on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are raising three school-aged daughters in Virginia.
