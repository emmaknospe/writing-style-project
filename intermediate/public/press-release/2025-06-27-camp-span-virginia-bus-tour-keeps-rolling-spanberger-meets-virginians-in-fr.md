FAIRFAX, Va. — On Thursday, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger’s “Span Virginia Bus Tour” continued with stops including Fredericksburg, Manassas Park, Fairfax County, and the City of Fairfax.

Throughout the day, Spanberger visited local businesses, met with union workers, and listened to Virginians discuss the issues that matter most to them and emphasized her commitment to always putting Virginia first — including through her “Affordable Virginia Plan” to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs.

FREDERICKSBURG

Spanberger began the sixth day of the “Span Virginia Bus Tour” with visits to several local businesses in Fredericksburg, listening to small business owners and Virginians about the issues that matter to them most. Spanberger heard stories of how the chaos coming out of Washington is hurting Main Street Virginia and made clear that as Virginia’s next Governor, she will always stand up for the best interests of Virginia and Virginians — regardless of who is in the White House.

MANASSAS PARK

Spanberger then headed to Manassas Park for a canvass kickoff with former Delegate Jay Jones, the Democratic nominee for Attorney General, and volunteers. At the event, Spanberger emphasized the importance of having meaningful conversations with friends and neighbors about the issues that matter most and the stakes of November’s election.

FAIRFAX COUNTY

Spanberger — along with state Senator Ghazala Hashmi, the Democratic nominee for Attorney General, and Jones — met with federal workers of SEIU Virginia 512 and made clear that as the next Governor of Virginia, she will always stand up for Virginia workers and Virginia jobs.

CITY OF FAIRFAX

Spanberger then led a rally in the City of Fairfax alongside Hashmi and Jones, outlining the stakes of this year’s election and her vision for Virginia’s future that will lower costs, strengthen the Commonwealth’s economy, make sure Virginia’s public schools are on a path to being the best in the country, and protect the fundamental freedoms of all Virginians.

BACKGROUND

Spanberger announced the launch of her “Span Virginia Bus Tour” last week at a rally at her high school alma mater, J.R. Tucker High School.

Along the “Span Virginia Bus Tour,” Spanberger is touting her plan to lower costs for Virginians — the “Affordable Virginia Plan.” Over the last month, she has held events across Virginia outlining this vision to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

Spanberger served three terms in the U.S. House of Representatives from January 2019 to January 2025. During her three terms in Congress, Spanberger was consistently ranked the most bipartisan Member of Congress from Virginia.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — working narcotics and money laundering cases. She then served as a CIA case officer — working on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters in Virginia public schools.
