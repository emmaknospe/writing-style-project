Ms. SPANBERGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor former Mayor Christopher Brown, a distinguished resident of Dumfries, Virginia, whose dedication and service have profoundly shaped his community.

Mr. Brown's roots run deep in Dumfries, where he has spent his life contributing to the town's growth and prosperity. As a former mayor, Mr. Brown played a pivotal role in shaping Dumfries' future, demonstrating exceptional leadership and vision during his tenure.

Beyond his public service, Mr. Brown continues to be a pillar of the community through his successful accounting firm. Established in 1987, his business has provided professional personal and business tax advice and preparation for more than three decades. His expertise and commitment to supporting local enterprises has been instrumental in fostering economic growth in Dumfries.

Mr. Brown's many contributions--as a lifelong resident, former mayor, and respected business owner--have left an indelible mark on the town. His accounting firm stands as a testament to his enduring commitment to the local business community.

Mr. Brown's unwavering dedication to Dumfries serves as an inspiration to all who strive to make a positive impact on their communities. His work ethic, business acumen, and public service exemplify the values we hold dear as Virginians.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing former Mayor Christopher Brown for his outstanding service and enduring commitment to the Town of Dumfries, Virginia. His legacy as a public servant, business leader, and admired community member will continue to influence and inspire future generations in Virginia.
