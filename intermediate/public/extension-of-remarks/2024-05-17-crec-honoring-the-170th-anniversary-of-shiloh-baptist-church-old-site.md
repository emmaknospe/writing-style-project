Ms. SPANBERGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and congratulate Shiloh Baptist Church Old Site on 170 years of service to the community.

This remarkable milestone is a testament to the thousands of congregants who, through their unwavering faith and love for their neighbors, have helped build a legacy of service at the church.

For 170 years, Shiloh Old Site has been an advocate for justice, equality, and perseverance. Throughout the years, this community has been a catalyst in halting the spread of racial injustice in the city of Fredericksburg and has worked tirelessly to provide supplemental education to Black residents to offset the shortfalls of a racially segregated educational system. Through its ministries and its purpose- driven youth mission, the church works to provide families and children with programming that nurtures a community service mindset.

Shiloh Old Site is a pillar of the Fredericksburg community--always ready to lend a helping hand to those in need. The community's commitment to Fredericksburg and its residents is both inspiring and humbling.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating and thanking Shiloh Baptist Church Old Site on 170 years of dedicated service to the Fredericksburg community. I look forward to celebrating their continued success and contributions to Virginia in years to come.
