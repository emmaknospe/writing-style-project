Ms. SPANBERGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Veterans of Foreign Wars Department of Virginia on their centennial celebration on March 4, 2023.

For 100 years, the Virginia VFW has worked hard to protect and fight for our veterans to ensure that they receive the benefits that they have earned and deserve. They support veterans, servicemembers, and their families by providing opportunities through education, scholarships, and living assistance.

I thank the VFW for their relentless advocacy and support of those who sacrificed for our country.

Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating the Veterans of Foreign Wars Department of Virginia on 100 years of service to our veterans and to making the Commonwealth of Virginia a better place for our veterans and their families.
