RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today released the following statement after Senator Ghazala Hashmi (SD-15) secured the Democratic nomination for Lieutenant Governor of Virginia.

“Tonight, I’d like to congratulate Senator Ghazala Hashmi on earning the Democratic nomination to be Virginia’s next Lieutenant Governor.

“This year, Virginia Democrats came out in record-breaking numbers to exercise their right to vote, participate in our democracy, and make their voices heard — and the results show clear enthusiasm for Senator Hashmi to serve as our next Lieutenant Governor. As a proven leader in the Virginia Senate, Senator Hashmi has passed legislation that’s delivered economic investment, improved schools, protected healthcare, and defended reproductive freedom. Our administration will be focused on making Virginia more affordable for all families, standing up for Virginians’ jobs and protecting Virginia’s workers, defending Virginians’ fundamental freedoms, and making sure Virginia’s schools are the best in the country — and we’ll get to work on day one.

“I’m looking forward to hitting the ground running this week and over the next five months with our entire Democratic ticket as we span Virginia — listening to the challenges facing our communities and winning Virginians’ trust that our administration will address them. And come January, we will work together to build a stronger Virginia for everyone who calls our Commonwealth home.”
