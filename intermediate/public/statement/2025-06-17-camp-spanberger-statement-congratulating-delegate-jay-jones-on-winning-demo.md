RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today released the following statement after former Delegate Jay Jones secured the Democratic nomination for Attorney General of Virginia.

“Congratulations to Delegate Jay Jones on being chosen by Virginians across the Commonwealth to serve as the Democratic nominee for Attorney General of Virginia.

“As politicians in Washington are threatening Virginians’ futures, Virginians deserve an Attorney General who will put them and their safety first. While Attorneys General in states across the country stand up for the people who call their states home, Virginia’s current Attorney General has declined to defend workers who are losing their jobs. I know that Delegate Jones will always work to keep Virginians safe, protect their communities, and defend Virginians’ fundamental rights — and he will never be afraid to stand up for Virginians and do what is right to keep our Commonwealth strong.

“Following in the footsteps of his father and grandfather, Delegate Jones has spent his life fighting for justice. As Attorney General, he’ll focus on keeping Virginians safe while protecting every Virginian’s rights and quality of life with the same fierce commitment to fairness and integrity that has defined his public service. As we work to earn Virginians’ trust — and their votes — this November, I look forward to joining together with our entire Democratic ticket to span the Commonwealth to make clear that our administration will protect Virginians’ safety, jobs, families, and communities.”
