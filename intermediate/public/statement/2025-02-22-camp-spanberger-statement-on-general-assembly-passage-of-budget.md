RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today released the following statement after majorities in both the House of Delegates and Virginia Senate approved a comprehensive budget — one that invests in every corner of the Commonwealth.

“Today, the General Assembly passed a budget that makes clear Democrats’ dedication to delivering for all Virginians. I’m grateful to Chair Lucas, Chair Torian, and their committees for their steadfast determination  to hear from and deliver for all Virginians in the budget process.  I applaud all who came together to pass a budget that  aims to cut costs for Virginia’s working families, strengthen Virginia’s schools, and invest in communities across the Commonwealth.”
