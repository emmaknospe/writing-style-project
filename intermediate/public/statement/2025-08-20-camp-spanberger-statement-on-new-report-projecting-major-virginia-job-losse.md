According to UVA’s Weldon Cooper Center, Virginia Will Have Virtually No Job Growth in 2026

Spanberger: “As Virginia’s Next Governor, I Will Do Everything in My Power to Get Virginia’s Economy Back on Track”

RICHMOND, Va — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today released the following statement in response to new reporting on a UVA Weldon Cooper Center analysis forecasting Virginia will have virtually no job growth in 2026 — due to the Trump Administration’s “delayed federal layoffs and postponed tariffs” that are hurting Virginia’s economy.

“This report confirms what I’m hearing directly from Virginia business leaders, workers, small business owners, farmers and producers, and families — that the Trump Administration’s reckless policies are stifling Virginia’s job market, driving up costs, and jeopardizing our Commonwealth’s long-term economic strength. While Virginians are hurting, my opponent is cheering on the White House and downplaying the reality of Virginians losing their jobs.

“Virginians deserve a Governor who will put Virginia’s economy first. As Virginia’s next Governor, I will do everything in my power to get Virginia’s economy back on track. I will work every day to foster a strong business climate that promotes growth and creates good-paying jobs. I will never be afraid to stand up for Virginia’s workers and Virginia’s economy when chaos in Washington is hurting our Commonwealth. And I will never put allegiance to a political party ahead of what’s best for Virginia.”

BACKGROUND

Last month, Spanberger announced her “Growing Virginia Plan,” her priorities for creating jobs and spurring economic growth — which include an emphasis on strengthening workforce training programs for Virginia students.

As a Member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to invest in Virginia’s infrastructure, grow trade opportunities for Virginia, and equip the next generation of workers with the tools they need to succeed.

In the U.S. House, Spanberger was consistently recognized for her leadership in supporting Virginia businesses and workers and supporting policies that advanced economic growth and helped create jobs.

In 2020 and 2022, Spanberger was endorsed by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce for her support and advocacy for Virginia’s business community in the U.S. House — including for her role in moving the bipartisan infrastructure law over the finish line. And in June 2020, she received the U.S. Chamber of Commerce’s first-ever “Jefferson-Hamilton Award for Bipartisanship” for her commitment to finding common ground on issues facing Virginia’s small businesses community and overall economy.

Earlier this month, the Spanberger for Governor campaign released a new TV ad highlighting Winsome Earle-Sears’s refusal to stand up to the Trump Administration’s attacks on Virginia jobs. When asked about her support for Trump’s disastrous purge of Virginia’s federal workers in a CNN interview, Sears dismissed the question — saying she would rather talk about “real issues.”
