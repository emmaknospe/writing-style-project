SPANBERGER: I'm Abigail Spanberger, candidate for governor, and I sponsored this ad.

NARRATOR: Maga Republican Winsome Earl Sears.

WINSOME EARLE-SPEARS: I AM SPEAKING.

NARRATOR: What does she say about Trump's bad budget bill?

WINSOME EARLE-SPEARS: The bill, as you know now, does so many great things.

NARRATOR: What? The Trump budget raises health care costs, raises mortgages, raises the price of electricity and gas.

You pay more so billionaires can pay less.

WINSOME EARLE-SPEARS: I AM SPEAKING.

NARRATOR: We've heard enough! Winsome Earle-Sears is so far right she’s wrong for Virginia.
