This Spanish Language Radio Ad Featuring Spanberger, Who Is Fluent in Spanish, Will Air Across Norfolk, Richmond, & DC stations

RICHMOND, Va. — Today, the Spanberger for Governor campaign released its first Spanish-language radio ad, highlighting Spanberger’s demonstrated focus on making Virginia more affordable and Winsome Earle-Sears’ record of raising costs on everyday Virginians.

The new radio ad titled, “Los Costos” highlights Winsome Earle-Sears’ vote for a sales tax on groceries and support for Donald Trump’s cuts to public education. The ad also focuses on Spanberger’s commitment to lowering costs for Virginia families.

Spanberger has previously announced her plan to lower Virginians’ healthcare and prescription drug, housing, and energy costs as part of her “Affordable Virginia Plan.” Last week, Spanberger also announced her “Strengthening Virginia Schools Plan” to strengthen Virginia’s child care and early childhood education programs, K-12 schools, and institutions of higher education.

Click here to listen to Spanberger’s new ad, and read a translation of the transcript of “Los Costos” below:

—

English Translation:

V/O [speaking Spanish]: Housing. Healthcare. Electricity. The price of everything keeps going up.

And if Winsome Earle-Sears had her way, costs would go even higher. Sears voted for a sales tax on groceries. A tax on groceries? C’mon.

And Sears backs Trump’s cuts to education and healthcare too.

We need a governor who fights for Virginia, not Trump.

Abigail Spanberger [speaking Spanish]: I’m Abigail Spanberger. As a mom of three girls in public school, I know what it’s like for families today.

I got things done to lower costs. That’s what I’ll do as Governor too.

Problems decades in the making can’t be solved overnight. But every journey begins with a few steps. I’ll take the steps to help you and your family get ahead.

V/O [speaking Spanish]: Abigail Spanberger. Service. Country. Commonwealth.
