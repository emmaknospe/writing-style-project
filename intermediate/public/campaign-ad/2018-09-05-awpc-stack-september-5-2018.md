SPANBERGER:
When we were getting ready to execute a search warrant or an arrest warrant, we'd line up and we'd put our hand on the shoulder of the person in front of us. You always knew who had your back. With all the money in politics, it's hard to say who has our backs. I worked in federal law enforcement and at CIA, serving the American people, not special interests. I'm Abigail's Bamberger. I won't take money from corporate PACs because in Congress I'll always have your back and that's why I approve this message.
