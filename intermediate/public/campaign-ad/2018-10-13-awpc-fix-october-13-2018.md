SPANBERGER:
I'm Abigail Spanberger. I've spent my career protecting America and enforcing the law. I don't support safe havens for criminals or gangs. Anyone who commits a violent crime should be prosecuted, and don't let anyone fool you I oppose abolishing ICE. I’ll work to strengthen our border security and fix our immigration system by creating an earned pathway to citizenship, especially for dreamers and active duty and military veterans. That's why I approve this message.
