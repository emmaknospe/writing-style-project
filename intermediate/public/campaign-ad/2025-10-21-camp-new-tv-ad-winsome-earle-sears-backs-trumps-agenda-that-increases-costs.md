RICHMOND, Va. —  The Spanberger for Governor campaign today released a new TV and digital ad calling out Winsome Earle-Sears for fully backing President Trump’s agenda that is raising housing costs for Virginia families and causing Virginia’s unemployment rate to skyrocket.

The ad, titled “Hurting,” highlights Sears’ praise for Trump’s policies — including his mass firings of Virginia workers, his administration’s reckless tariffs, and the tax law Trump signed in July — that is making life more expensive for Virginians. Earlier this year, when discussing her stance on the President’s cost-spiking and job-killing agenda, Sears said “I’m supporting what the President is doing.”

While Sears continues to back the Trump Administration’s disastrous economic policies that caused Virginia’s unemployment rate to rise for seven straight months, Spanberger has announced her plans to lower costs for Virginia families, protect Virginia jobs, and grow Virginia’s economy. Earlier this year, Spanberger rolled out her plan to lower costs for Virginians across the board — her Affordable Virginia Plan, which includes her priorities to make Virginia the nationwide leader in addressing the housing affordability crisis and help more Virginia families build a life in the Commonwealth.
