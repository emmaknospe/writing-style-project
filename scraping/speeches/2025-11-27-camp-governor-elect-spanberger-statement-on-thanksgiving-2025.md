Governor-Elect Spanberger: “From My Family to Yours, I Wish All Virginians a Joyous, Safe, and Restful Thanksgiving”

RICHMOND, Va. — Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger today released the following statement wishing Virginians a happy Thanksgiving and encouraging them to give back to their communities during the holiday season.

“This holiday, Virginians across the Commonwealth will gather with their friends, families, and loved ones as we give thanks for all we have been blessed with over the past year.

“We are especially grateful to the servicemembers, National Guardsmen, first responders, law enforcement officers, and healthcare workers who keep our communities safe — especially during the holiday season when doing so takes time away from celebrating with their loved ones. We also give thanks to the Virginia farmers and producers who work tirelessly to feed families across the Commonwealth and the country.

“However, for many families across the Commonwealth, we know the holiday season is a painful reminder of those they have lost. I encourage all Virginians to embrace the holiday spirit and extend warmth and kindness to their friends, families, and neighbors who are struggling — uplifting those who may be in need during a difficult time of year.

“I am incredibly grateful for the faith and trust my fellow Virginians have put in me to serve as your next Governor. From my family to yours, I wish all Virginians a joyous, safe, and restful Thanksgiving.”

—

Spanberger and members of her family spent the day on Wednesday preparing Thanksgiving meals with The Giving Heart in Richmond for families in need across Central Virginia.
