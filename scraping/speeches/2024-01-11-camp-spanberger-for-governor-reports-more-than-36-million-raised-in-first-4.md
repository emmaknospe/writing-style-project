Spanberger’s Campaign Received More Than 9,200 Contributions of $100 or Less — More than Any Previous Virginia Gubernatorial Campaign in its First Filing

Spanberger Has Received Contributions from All 133 Virginia Localities

HENRICO, Va. — Abigail Spanberger for Governor today announced that the campaign raised more than $3.62 million from nearly 12,800 contributions from November 13 to December 31, 2023 — just 48 days.

U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger’s campaign is powered by grassroots donors across Virginia:

“Since day one of this campaign, thousands of Virginians across the Commonwealth — from all 133 localities — have been excited about Abigail Spanberger serving as Virginia’s next Governor,” said Sam Signori, Campaign Manager, Abigail Spanberger for Governor. “We are tremendously grateful for this outpouring of support — and this early momentum demonstrates the true, grassroots enthusiasm and energy for Abigail’s campaign. Virginians know that Abigail will bring people together as Governor to support our schools, grow Virginia’s economy, keep families safe, and protect Virginians’ fundamental rights.”

Beyond record-breaking fundraising numbers, Spanberger is off to an early start in earning key Virginia endorsements. Since launching her campaign, Spanberger has been endorsed by former Virginia Governor Ralph Northam; U.S. Representatives Jennifer Wexton (VA-10), Gerry Connolly (VA-11), and Don Beyer (VA-08); former U.S. Representatives Elaine Luria (VA-02) and Tom Perriello (VA-05); and dozens of state and local officials from across the Commonwealth.

Click here for a list of Virginia leaders who’ve endorsed Spanberger’s campaign.

Spanberger has also been endorsed by Emily’s List, VoteVets, and Brady PAC.

Abigail Spanberger announced her campaign to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia in November 2023.
