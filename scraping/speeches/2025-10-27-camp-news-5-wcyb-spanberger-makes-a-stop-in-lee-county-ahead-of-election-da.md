By Liam Bridgeman

LEE COUNTY, Va. (WCYB) — Virginia gubernatorial candidate Abigail Spanberger made a stop in Lee County, VA Monday to speak on topics fueling her campaign.

Spanberger’s visit began at the Lee County Community Center where she spoke to voters about the issues that she sees at the forefront for Virginians.

“It’s an extraordinary time and people across the commonwealth they’re facing real challenges, whether it’s rising cost of housing or healthcare or energy,” Spanberger said.

One of the biggest topics Spanberger has consistently denounced along the campaign trail is the ‘One Big Beautiful Bill’ which was signed into law by President Trump on July 4. It contains sweeping changes to SNAP and Medicaid, including changes to hospital funding.

“We need a governor who is going to plan meticulously to mitigate every harm that will come from that bill and I will be honest, that bill is going to be dire in terms of the impact that it has on healthcare all across Virginia,” Spanberger said.

While in Lee County, Spanberger toured the Lee County Community Hospital, which has been the focal point for several lawmakers, on both sides of the aisle, since the bill was passed.

Ballad Health has previously told News 5, if nothing changes, the system will lose hundreds of millions of dollars because of the bill.

“As governor, among the things that I can do is work to ensure that those who get their healthcare through Medicaid and need to be able to prove that they are working and working hard, that we can put every structure in place to ensure they can meet that requirement to stay on Medicaid,” Spanberger said.

“To be able to work relentlessly to push for changes that might undo some of the worst potential outcomes from that legislation is something that I will do as governor.”

Early voting is set to end on November 1 and election day is on November 4.
