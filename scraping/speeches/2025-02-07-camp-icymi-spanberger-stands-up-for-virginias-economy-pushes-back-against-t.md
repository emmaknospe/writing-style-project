Spanberger: “This Is a Kitchen-Table Issue in Virginia”

Virginia is Home to More Than 140,000 Federal Employees

RICHMOND, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger last night spoke with MSNBC’s Ali Velshi about the Trump Administration’s attacks against federal government employees who call Virginia home — and made clear her commitment to protecting Virginia’s workforce and economy.

Virginia is home to more than 140,000 federal employees. In last night’s interview, Spanberger spoke out against the Trump Administration’s threatening of nonpartisan career public servants and made clear the wide-ranging consequences for Virginia’s workforce and economy. She also underscored the wide-ranging impacts of President Trump’s proposed freeze of all federal funding, which has caused community health centers in Virginia to close locations, cut offered services, and cancel patient appointments.

“Let me tell you who some of these people are, because they are Virginians — so many of them.

They are the nurse at the VA who takes care of our nation’s heroes.

They are the Social Security employee who ensures that our grandparents get their Social Security checks.

They are the law enforcement officers who keep our country safe from terrorist threats the world over.

They are the intelligence community members who spend every single day working to keep their neighbors safe.

They are the air traffic controllers who work day in and day out to avoid tragedy.

They are moms and dads and community members, and they are Virginians.

And this is, yes, about them. And for them, it’s a kitchen-table issue. But the impact, the idea that we would have a President and a Governor who is standing by as said President says we’re going to push them out of the workforce? I mean, this is a kitchen-table issue in Virginia.”
