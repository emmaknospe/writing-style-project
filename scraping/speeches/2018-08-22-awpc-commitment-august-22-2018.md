SPANBERGER:
So before I was with the CIA, I was in federal law enforcement. I worked with other federal and local agencies, we worked drug cases and Child Exploitation and we work together because American lives were on the line and because the people we served came first. And I'm running for Congress now because I believe that we need people who are committed to service. Understanding what a profound responsibility it is to swear an oath to uphold the Constitution. I'm Abigail Spanberger and I approve this message.
