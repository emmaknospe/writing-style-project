By Jessica Maldonado

ROANOKE, Va. (WFXR)- Democratic nominee for governor, Abigail Spanberger, is continuing her eight-day, 40-stop “Span Virginia” bus tour across the commonwealth.

With just 89 days until early voting begins, the bus rolled into the Virginia Museum of Transportation in Roanoke, as local supporters gathered.

Spanberger took the stage to highlight the key priorities of her campaign. Those include strengthening Virginia’s economy and expanding affordable housing through her newly unveiled housing affordability agenda.

Which she rolled out just a couple weeks ago.

She spoke on protecting reproductive rights and improving healthcare access across the commonwealth. But Spanberger says she’s looking to make a change.

“I do not accept what I am seeing and therefore I will work to change it. I am working for, what we are working for is a Virginia that is one that our kids deserve. One that we will be proud to leave to the next generation,” Spanberger said.

Her fellow democratic leaders spoke on issues and emphasized Virginia not going backwards.

“We are going to protect our federal workers, we are going to protect our veterans, we are going to protect every vulnerable community that is about to be impacted by that chaos from D.C ,” said lieutenant governor candidate Ghazala Hashmi.

Spanberger plans to look into every challenge and reaffirms her commitment to bipartisan leadership.

She stated she built coalitions while serving in congress and plans to do the same if elected governor.
