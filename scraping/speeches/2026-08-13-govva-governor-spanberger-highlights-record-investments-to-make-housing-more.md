RICHMOND, VA — Governor Abigail Spanberger today joined Virginians in Arlington to highlight the Commonwealth’s record investments to lower housing costs and celebrate the recent opening of Wholey Legacy Homes — a newly constructed 234-unit apartment complex.

Together with Senator Barbara Favola and additional local leaders, the Governor toured the new units that create more options for families at 60% AMI or below. The Governor also hosted a discussion with community partners about the long-term affordability of Northern Virginia’s communities, where she pledged to continue her push to lower housing costs across the Commonwealth.

“Every Virginian should be able to find a home that meets their needs, fits their budget, and allows their family to thrive,” said Governor Abigail Spanberger. “This beautiful new building is a testament to the important work being done to make that a reality, and I’m proud of the record new investments in our state budget to continue that progress. Housing is the foundation of a strong community and a strong economy, and we will keep working to ensure that Virginians have access to safe, affordable homes and the opportunity to build a better future.”

“Durable economic growth, good jobs, and affordable housing are inextricably linked,” said Secretary of Commerce and Trade Carrie Chenery. “By expanding housing options right next to major transit hubs in Arlington, we are giving workers an affordable place to call home while giving Northern Virginia businesses access to the talent they need to thrive. This state investment ensures regional economies and communities continue to grow from the ground up.”

Virginia’s biennial budget includes nearly $100 million to expand housing development and help more Virginians find housing that they can actually afford to rent or buy. This investment includes record funding for the Virginia Housing Trust Fund, which is dedicated to creating, preserving, and supporting more affordable housing — like Wholey Legacy Homes — for Virginians.

“True Ground and its many partnerships, including with the Arlington government, has created a model that supports residents in achieving an affordable life,” said Senator Barbara Favola. “This model should be replicated across the Commonwealth.”

“The partnership between state and local governments is crucial for delivering affordable housing to those who need it,” said Arlington County Board Chair Matt de Ferranti. “All Virginians deserve the chance to achieve the American Dream and expand opportunity for themselves and their families.”

“Virginia Housing is proud to support developments that expand access to safe, stable, and affordable housing,” said CEO of Virginia Housing Tammy Neale. “We know that when we invest in housing that people can afford, we strengthen families and the communities they call home. We are grateful for the collaboration of state and local partners who share our commitment to making sure every Virginian has a safe, affordable place to live.”

“DHCD’s $7.6 million investment in Wholey Legacy Homes demonstrates what is possible when strategic funding and forward-thinking design come together,” said Director of the Department of Housing and Community Development Tamarah Holmes, Ph.D. “By pairing deeply affordable homes with universal design and green building standards, we are creating safe, accessible, and energy-efficient spaces where residents of all ages and abilities can thrive in the heart of Arlington.”

“We are grateful to Governor Spanberger for recognizing Wholey Legacy Homes as a powerful example of how combining quality affordable housing with resident services can strengthen communities,” said President & CEO of True Ground Housing Partners Carmen Romero. “The Governor’s visit highlights the positive impact developments like Wholey Legacy Homes have on families and neighborhoods, and we are proud to showcase the cross-sector partnerships that make projects like this possible.”

In June, the Governor signed a package of legislation aimed at cutting red tape that prevents housing development, protecting Virginians from hidden fees in rental agreements, and easing the burdens facing first-time homebuyers. Additionally, the Governor signed her entire Affordable Virginia Agenda aimed at lowering housing, healthcare, and energy costs across the Commonwealth.
