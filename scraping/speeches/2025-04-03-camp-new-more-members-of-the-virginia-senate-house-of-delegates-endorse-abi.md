RICHMOND, Va. — Today, additional members of the Virginia Senate and House of Delegates endorsed Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia.

Today’s endorsements reflect the expanding coalition of support Spanberger is building across the Commonwealth.

The following Virginia Senators and House of Delegates members (listed by district number) today endorsed Spanberger for Governor:

Sen. Creigh Deeds, Virginia Senate, District 11

Sen. Lamont Bagby, Virginia Senate, District 14

Sen. Angelia Williams Graves, Virginia Senate, District 21

Sen. Dave Marsden, Virginia Senate, District 35

Sen. Saddam Azlan Salim, Virginia Senate, District 37

Sen. Jennifer Boysko, Virginia Senate, District 38

Del. Adele McClure, Virginia House of Delegates, District 2

Del. Dan Helmer, Virginia House of Delegates, District 10

Del. Michelle Maldonado, Virginia House of Delegates, District 20

Del. JJ Singh, Virginia House of Delegates, District 26

Del. Katrina Callsen, Virginia House of Delegates, District 54

Del. Amy Laufer, Virginia House of Delegates, District 55

Del. Shelly Simonds, Virginia House of Delegates, District 70

Del. Destiny LeVere Bolling, Virginia House of Delegates, District 80

Del. Delores McQuinn, Virginia House of Delegates, District 81

Del. Cliff Hayes, Virginia House of Delegates, District 91

Del. Bonita Anthony, Virginia House of Delegates, District 92

“I am grateful for the support of General Assembly members from every corner of our Commonwealth,” said Spanberger. “Our broad coalition shows Virginians are ready to put an end to chaos and focus on policies that improve Virginians’ daily lives — lowering the cost of living, strengthening our schools, and expanding opportunities for home ownership. I look forward to working with the General Assembly to build a brighter future for all Virginians — because everyone who works hard and plays by the rules deserves to have a strong future here in our Commonwealth.”

BACKGROUND

Spanberger served three terms in the U.S. House of Representatives from January 2019 to January 2025. Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer —  working narcotics and money laundering cases. She then served as a CIA case officer — working on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases. She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters in Virginia public schools.

Abigail Spanberger announced her campaign to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia in November 2023.
