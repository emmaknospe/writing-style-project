By Timothy Cama

Virginia Democratic gubernatorial hopeful Abigail Spanberger is focusing on technologies like solar energy and battery storage in her new energy platform.

Spanberger, a former House member hoping to flip control of the governor’s mansion and succeed Gov. Glenn Youngkin (R), is also putting an emphasis on the growing data center sector in Virginia, with a goal of making sure the facilities and their energy demand do not lead to increased electricity prices.

“My agenda is focused on expanding generation, ensuring that we are strengthening our grid, planning for a future where Virginia’s energy generation is not just about utilizing renewable energy and technologies of the future, but also about lowering costs and bringing more jobs to Virginia,” she said in a video announcing her plan Monday in Norfolk, where she visited a resident who had recently completed weatherization upgrades to her home.

“As the next Governor of Virginia, I will be focused on lowering costs and making Virginians’ bills more affordable — because I know that affordable energy is essential to driving economic growth,” Spanberger said in a statement.

“At a time when the federal government is trying to raise Virginians’ energy costs — including by slashing LIHEAP funding and cutting wind and solar tax credits that help us build affordable energy right now — and threatening local jobs, we need a Governor who will make energy more affordable for Virginia consumers,” she said.

Under Spanberger’s plan, the state would put a new focus on “local” electricity generation, likely putting solar in new places. She would also seek to expand offshore wind, nuclear power, geothermal and hydrogen.

The Democrat says she would focus on energy conservation through practices like weatherization, and expanding energy storage to make better use of resources.

Spanberger wants data centers to pay their “fair share” of new generation costs, and she would encourage them to use clean energy. She also wants to return the state to the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative, which Youngkin pulled the state out of.

Spanberger is running against Republican Lt. Gov. Winsome Earle-Sears, since Youngkin is term limited. Spanberger has the support of organizations including the Virginia League of Conservation Voters and Clean Virginia.
