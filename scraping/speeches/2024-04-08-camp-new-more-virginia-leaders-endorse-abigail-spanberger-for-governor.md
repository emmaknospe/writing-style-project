HENRICO COUNTY, Va. – Today, more Virginia leaders endorsed U.S. Representative Abigail Spanberger to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia.

These new endorsements from current and former Virginia elected officials from across the Commonwealth reflect growing support for Spanberger’s campaign to serve as Virginia’s next Governor.

The following Virginia leaders today endorsed Abigail Spanberger to serve as Virginia’s next Governor:

BACKGROUND

Spanberger has also been endorsed by Emily’s List, VoteVets, and Brady PAC.

In November 2023, U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger (VA-07) announced her campaign to serve as the 75th Governor of Virginia.

Spanberger is serving her third term in the U.S. House of Representatives. In Congress, she serves on the U.S. House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence and the U.S. House Agriculture Committee.

Before Congress, Spanberger served as a federal law enforcement officer — tracking narcotics traffickers and working money laundering cases — and as a CIA case officer — working undercover on counterterrorism and nuclear counterproliferation cases.

She and her husband Adam are the proud parents of three school-aged daughters.
