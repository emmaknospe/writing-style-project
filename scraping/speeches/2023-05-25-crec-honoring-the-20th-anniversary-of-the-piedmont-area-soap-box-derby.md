Ms. SPANBERGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate the Piedmont Area Soap Box Derby on its 20th anniversary. Since June 27, 2003, Virginians have participated in this fun and family-friendly event that started as a 75th anniversary celebration of Rosson & Troilo, a family-owned and operated business. The inaugural race in Culpeper, Virginia began with a total of 35 drivers and was awarded ``Outstanding New Race City'' by the All-American World Championship Race.

In 2005, the Piedmont Area Soap Box Derby piloted the first-ever Junior Committee for a soap box derby. Members of the Junior Committee are between 13 and 21 years of age, and they take on the same responsibilities and goals as the adult committee. The Junior Committee operates as a mentorship program, providing members with leadership opportunities and allowing young derby drivers to learn from seasoned drivers in the community.

By 2007, the Piedmont Area Soap Box Derby was ranked ``2nd largest derby in the world.'' In 2008, they claimed the title of ``largest derby in the world.'' In 2009, with the help of the community through donations and volunteers, they began building a permanent track facility, the Paul Bates Raceway. Since 2003, Rosson & Troilo has made it possible for derby champions and their families to travel to Akron, Ohio for the All-American World Championship Race. Throughout the last 20 years, Piedmont Area Soap Box Derby drivers have brought home many awards, including five world championships--Brandon Feagan (2008), Sean Brown (2010), Gabrielle Beville (2011), Joey Kratochvil (2014), and Mason Breedon (2016).

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the Piedmont Area Soap Box Derby for 20 years of providing Virginia with a wonderful event that promotes community, leadership, and encourages young and seasoned drivers towards success. I look forward to all that they accomplish in the years to come.
