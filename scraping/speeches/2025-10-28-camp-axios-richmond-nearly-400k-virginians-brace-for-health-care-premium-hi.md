“Out-of-Pocket Costs Could be Two to More than Five Times Higher”

Earlier This Year, Spanberger Announced Her Plan to Lower Virginians’ Healthcare & Prescription Drug Costs

RICHMOND, Va. — One week until Election Day, Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger continues to stand up for Virginians’ healthcare as President Trump’s tax law — which her opponent Winsome Earle-Sears has praised — threatens to skyrocket healthcare costs for Virginians.

New reporting from Axios Richmond underscores the devastating impacts of Trump’s reckless policies — and Republicans’ refusal to extend the enhanced premium tax credits — for the nearly 400,000 Virginians who are covered on the Marketplace through the Affordable Care Act (ACA).

With open enrollment on Virginia’s Marketplace beginning this Saturday, Virginians will face higher costs for the coverage they rely on. According to the State Corporation Commission (SCC), out-of-pocket costs could be two to more than five times higher for these families and the projected hikes could push hundreds of thousands of people into the uninsured pool. The agency said last week it began to mail renewal notices to Virginians with up to 40% premium increases.

Yesterday, Spanberger toured Lee County Community Hospital to discuss the critical care they provide for Virginians in rural communities and learn more about the threats to rural providers — and the Virginians’ they serve — because of Trump’s tax law. Spanberger made clear that, as the next Governor of Virginia, she will stand up to attacks on rural healthcare providers and work to lower Virginians’ healthcare and prescription drug costs.

“Over the next week, Abigail Spanberger will be spending every single day talking about her vision for putting Virginia first, including standing up for the healthcare of Virginia families,” said Connor Joseph, Communications Director, Spanberger for Governor. “While Abigail is focused on protecting access to affordable healthcare coverage and lowering costs at the pharmacy counter, Winsome Earle-Sears continues to cheer on President Trump’s disastrous policies that are causing Virginians’ healthcare premiums to spike.”

While Spanberger has repeatedly spoken out against Trump’s tax law that raises healthcare costs and jeopardizes healthcare access for over 350,000 Virginians, Sears has backed Trump’s cuts to Virginians’ healthcare at every turn. Sears has repeatedly praised the GOP law, claiming it “does so many great things.”

BACKGROUND

Earlier this year, Spanberger released her Affordable Virginia Plan which is focused on lowering Virginians’ healthcare and prescription drug costs by cracking down on industry middlemen that drive up costs, improving pricing transparency, holding large pharmaceutical companies accountable, and expanding  healthcare access in rural and underserved communities.

Spanberger has a proven record of lowering costs for Virginia families and seniors. During her three terms in Congress, Spanberger helped lead the charge to give Medicare the power to negotiate for lower drug prices and save taxpayers billions of dollars in the process.

Spanberger also voted to cap insulin copays at $35 per month for seniors on Medicare and cap out-of-pocket drug costs for Virginia seniors. Additionally, President Trump signed into law Spanberger’s effort to make it harder for pharmaceutical industry middlemen — pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs) — to rip off consumers.
