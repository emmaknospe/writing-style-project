President Barack Obama: “As Governor, Abigail Will Stand up for Virginia Families. She’ll Work to Build an Economy that Works for Everyone”

RICHMOND, Va. — President Barack Obama today endorsed Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger in her campaign to serve as the next Governor of Virginia.

In two new digital ads targeted to Virginia voters, President Obama highlights Spanberger’s commitment to lowering costs and always putting Virginians first — particularly as Trump and Republicans in Congress support policies that raise healthcare, mortgage, and electricity costs. The ads also highlight Spanberger’s record of protecting Virginians’ fundamental rights — including their voting rights and the right of women to make their own healthcare decisions.
