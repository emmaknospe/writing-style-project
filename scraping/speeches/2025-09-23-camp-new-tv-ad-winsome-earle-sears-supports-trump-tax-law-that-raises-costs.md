Winsome Earle-Sears Said the GOP Tax Bill That Cuts Healthcare Coverage for Hundreds of Thousands of Virginians “Does So Many Great Things”

In Contrast, Spanberger Has Been Laser-Focused on Her Plans to Make Virginia More Affordable

RICHMOND, Va. — The Spanberger for Governor campaign today released a new TV and digital ad highlighting Winsome Earle-Sears’ support for Donald Trump’s tax law that will raise costs for Virginians.

The new ad, titled “What About You” highlights how Sears signed a special interest pledge not to raise taxes on billionaires and corporations, as well as her praise for the Republican tax bill Donald Trump signed into law earlier this year. Sears said the law — which is projected to raise Virginians’ healthcare costs, mortgage payments, and utility bills — “does so many great things.”

As Sears continues to put loyalty to Donald Trump ahead of what’s best for the Commonwealth, Spanberger has been outspoken about the consequences of this law for Virginians — including the estimated hundreds of thousands of Virginians who will lose their healthcare coverage. As Republicans try to make life more expensive for Virginia families, Spanberger is focused on making Virginia more affordable. Earlier this year, she announced her “Affordable Virginia Plan,” which lays out the concrete ways that her administration will get to work on day one on day one to lower Virginians’ healthcare and prescription drug, housing, and energy costs.
