SPANBERGER:
Washington is broken, our country is divided, and Congress is too partisan. At the CIA, I served under Republicans and Democrats. Country came first. Changing Washington means choosing new leaders of both parties. That's why I won't support Nancy Pelosi or Paul Ryan and it's why it's time for representatives who refuse to work across the aisle like Congressman Dave Brat to go. I'm Abigail Spanberger. I approve this message because we need to put country before party.
