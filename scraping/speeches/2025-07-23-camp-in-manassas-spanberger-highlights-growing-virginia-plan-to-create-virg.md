Amid Threats to Virginia Jobs & Virginia’s Economy, Spanberger’s “Growing Virginia Plan” Prioritizes Growing Workforce Training Paths, Business Investment, & Trade Opportunities

Spanberger: “Virginia Deserves a Governor Who Is Focused on Building an Economy Where Every Virginian Can Earn a Good Living”

MANASSAS, Va. — Congresswoman Abigail Spanberger today in Northern Virginia laid out her new “Growing Virginia Plan,” which is focused on growing Virginia’s economy and creating job opportunities for Virginians across every corner of the Commonwealth.

Spanberger outlined her plan just days after new data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics showed that Virginia was the only state to see its unemployment rate rise in June. Earlier this month, Virginia lost its #1 ranking on CNBC’s “America’s Top States for Business” rankings — due in part to a drop in the state’s “Economy” ranking and the Trump Administration’s federal job cuts “disproportionately” impacting Virginia’s economy.

At IBEW Local Union 26 in Manassas, Spanberger laid out a new slate of policy priorities focused on growing Virginia’s economy and workforce training opportunities for the next generation of Virginians. Spanberger’s “Growing Virginia Plan” is made up of three pillars — growing Virginia’s workforce, growing business investment in Virginia, and growing Virginia’s trade opportunities. During the event, Spanberger also spoke to the Trump Administration’s threats to Virginia’s economy and Virginians’ jobs — and how she will be a Governor who puts Virginia first.

“It’s not hyperbole to say that Virginia’s economy is under threat right now. The Trump Administration’s DOGE cuts and tariffs are threatening Virginia’s workers, businesses, families, and overall economy. Virginia needs a Governor who will not casually dismiss these threats to our economy — and who actually has a plan to build a more resilient economy,” said Spanberger during the event. “Virginia deserves a Governor who is focused on building an economy where every Virginian can earn a good living, take care of their families, and know they’ll have a secure retirement.”

Spanberger continued, “I promise you — we will get things done and we will put Virginia’s economy first. Come January, Virginia will have a Governor who is laser-focused on lowering costs for Virginia families and focused on building an economy that brings new opportunities to Virginia’s workers, businesses, and communities.”

Spanberger was joined by Chris Cash, business manager for IBEW Local Union 26, who shared how IBEW is working to train the next generation of Virginia workers — and how Spanberger will support Virginia’s workforce training and apprenticeship programs as Governor.

“Here at this facility, IBEW has helped thousands of students develop the skills they need to enter the workforce, earn a good living, and take care of their families. Our programs are about more than training and certifications — they’re about setting people up for success in good-paying, stable careers. But we need a Governor who is ready to support the workforce training and education programs that are growing our economy,” said Cash. “Abigail Spanberger has always made sure Virginia workers always have a seat at the table — and I know she will be a champion for the next generation in Richmond.”

Spanberger was also joined by Delegate Josh Thomas (HD-21), who spoke about how Spanberger will be a Governor who prioritizes Virginia’s economy, protects Virginians’ jobs, and stands up to threats coming out of Washington.

“Over the past two years, I’ve been proud to work alongside my colleagues in the General Assembly to help build an economy that works for more Virginians. Abigail Spanberger is the only candidate for Governor with a real plan to create good-paying jobs for Virginians, attract new investment to Virginia, and support Virginia’s businesses,” said Thomas. “Abigail will get to work on day one as Governor to tackle the everyday challenges facing Virginia’s economy and stand up against threats to Virginia’s federal workers, federal contractors, and small businesses. While her opponent is cheering on the White House as Virginians lose their jobs — including right here in Manassas, Abigail is speaking out.”

BACKGROUND

Spanberger has long prioritized lowering costs for Virginia families and protecting the Commonwealth’s economy.

After Virginia fell from its spot as “America’s Top State for Business” — due in part to the outsized impact of the Trump Administration’s federal job cuts on Virginia’s economy, Spanberger made clear her commitment to protecting Virginia’s economy as the next Governor of Virginia.

Earlier this year, Spanberger announced her Affordable Virginia Plan to lower healthcare, housing, and energy costs for Virginia families.

As a Member of Congress, Spanberger built a record of working with both Democrats and Republicans to invest in Virginia’s physical infrastructure — and has been a champion of policies that have created more jobs for Virginians, including the bipartisan infrastructure law.
